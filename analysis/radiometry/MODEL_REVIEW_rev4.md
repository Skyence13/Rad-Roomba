# Review of `MODEL.md` revision 4, §3 "Where U_null comes from", by the radiometry analyst

Scope: the null-reference window (§3), the time at which a cell's decision is evaluated, the reference of stationary recounts, the search-stage ratemeter, and the `alpha_short` question (review `docs/reviews/REVIEW_head_data_to_map.md` C1, M2, M6). `MODEL.md` was not edited. Evidence: `report/model_rev4_unull.md` (generated by `scripts/s8_unull.py`, run by `run_all.sh`), `results/rev4_*.csv`, `rrrad/unull.py`, `tests/test_unull.py`, and the probe of the ros2_ws processing `scripts/probe_unull_windows.py` → `results/rev4_window_probe.csv` (not re-run by `run_all.sh`). Everything is **calculated** (exact lattice convolution summed over the Poisson window count) or **simulated** (scenarios) with **synthetic** coefficients; nothing is measured.

## Verdict

The 200·T_F rule is stated in the wrong quantity and should be replaced. The threshold needs a **number of reference counts**, which depends on α (and on how strongly the null variance depends on U), not on T_F. The rule over-demands for slow or multi-pass cells (T_F 1–6 s need 200–1200 s of reference) and under-demands for edge cells (decided on fewer than 50 counts). With a **predictive threshold** (null distribution averaged over the posterior of U given the window), 30–200 counts hold achieved/nominal ≤ 1.05 everywhere in the grid down to α = 1.14e-5. Every cell of every probed scenario then has a window.

## 1. Why mid-run cells had no window (task 1)

Probe of `rr_core` (`results/rev4_window_probe.csv`; "live" = the last evaluation during the run, which is what is exported; "final" = one evaluation of every cell at the end with all data):

| Scenario (duration) | Cells | No lane decidable: live export (rev 4) | Final evaluation (rev 4 rule) | Cells with < 100 / < 200 counts in the attachment record, lane or ratio-scaled pool (proposed rule) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| s02 head resets (230 s, 3 attachment series) | 231 | 32 | 9 | 0 / 3 |
| s03 protective stop (231 s) | 231 | 27 | 0 | 0 / 0 |
| s04a blocked strip (461 s) | 405 | 28 | 0 | 0 / 0 |
| s10 step-and-count (406 s) | 133 | 17 | 0 | 0 / 0 |
| s15 (46 s) | 44 | 40 | 28 | 0 / 0 |

Under the proposed rule, the expected fraction of undecidable cells is **0** in every probed scenario at α ≥ 10⁻³, including the 46 s run. At mission-level α (N_min = 200), 3 of 231 cells (1.3 %) of s02 remain undecidable, all in the 14.6 s attachment series after a head reset. The processing engineer's "about 12 %" was mostly the live-evaluation artefact: 27–40 cells live against 0–9 after a final evaluation. Three causes, two of them in the implementation:
1. **Evaluation schedule (implementation, review C1).** A cell is re-evaluated only when it gains gates, using the reference data received so far. The window is truncated at "now" and frozen into the export. It accounts for 23 of 32 undecidable cells in s02 and all of them in s03, s04 and s10.
2. **Window assembly (implementation).** The window is ±100·T_F around each gate under test, within one attachment's series. A head reset starts a new series, and the window is not extended to the side that has data. Every failing lane (100 %) lies within half a window of a series start or end; "not near the trailer ends" is because series ends are in time, not in position.
3. **Requirement in seconds ∝ T_F (rule).** It cannot be met in a 46 s run (it needs 50 s per lane of pooled reference) even with a final evaluation. Yet the failing lanes held 181–812 counts, which is far more than the threshold needs.

## 2. What the threshold needs (task 2)

The statistic's null mean is zero for any U; U_null sets only the null shape. To second order the plug-in inflation is exp(z⁴g²/(8N)), with N the window count and g = d ln Var₀/d ln U (≈ 1, up to 1.7 for long recounts at 3 µSv/h, where U²u²(k) dominates). Exact window counts for achieved/nominal ≤ 1.05 (≤ 1.10 in brackets), lane window, beta window:

| α | Plug-in, scan 1 s | Plug-in, recount 10–60 s | Predictive, scan | Predictive, recount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | ≤ 35 | ≤ 73 | ≤ 25 | ≤ 25 |
| 0.01 | ≤ 108 | ≤ 267 | ≤ 29 | ≤ 37 |
| 1e-3 | ≤ 312 | ≤ 767 | ≤ 49 | ≤ 71 |
| 1.71e-4 (demo α_recount) | — | ≤ 1400 | — | ≤ 100 |
| 1.14e-5 (mission) | ≤ 1001 (512) | ≤ 2700 (1371) | ≤ 99 (50) | ≤ 175 (91) |

- A one-sided window is exactly as precise as a centred one with the same count. The two differ only under field variation (section 4).
- **Pooled window:** a lane whose reference reads 1 % above the four-lane mean gets 1.10–1.12 × nominal at mission α; 2 %: 1.15–1.22; 5 %: 1.41–1.60. Pooling is valid only when scaled by each lane's reference-rate ratio, estimated over the attachment, with that ratio's statistical uncertainty in the posterior. Even then, a lateral field difference across the 0.26 m head is a residual bias, so the lane's own window comes first.
- Independence from the gates under test matters because of correlation, not precision: the same-gate pool fails at any length.

## 3. Treating the U_null uncertainty (task 3)

- **Window count as a term of the statistic.** Subtracting c·N_window (in place of, or as well as, the same-gate reference) also gives a null mean independent of U, and a lower variance. But it compares the floor with reference counts taken elsewhere or at another time, which re-introduces the field-variation sensitivity that compensation exists to remove. The variance shape still depends on U. Rejected.
- **Exact conditional test** (binomial given N_F + N_ref): exact only with b = 0, equal weights and no calibration term. Not applicable.
- **Predictive threshold (recommended).** t* solves E_{U'}[P(S > t* | U')] = α, with U' ~ Gamma(N_w + ½, T_w), the Jeffreys posterior given the window. In the Gil-Pelaez evaluator this is the average of the characteristic functions over 12 posterior nodes (Gauss-Legendre in probability). Exact achieved/nominal: ≤ 1.05 at the counts in the table and ≤ 1.10 at 50 counts for scans at mission α (plug-in needs 512). Cost in D#: ≤ 1.07 at 50 counts, ≤ 1.04 at 100, ≤ 1.01 at 400 against U known.
- **Provably conservative alternative:** U at the upper (1 − α/10) Poisson bound, threshold at 0.9α. It holds α with achieved/nominal 0.03–0.4, but costs more in D# (`results/rev4_cost.csv`). Not recommended.

## 4. Field gradients (task 4)

Window of 175 counts at 0.1 m/s: lane window 5.3 m of travel at 0.1 µSv/h (0.53 m at 1 µSv/h); pooled 1.3 m (0.13 m). The statistic is unbiased (same-gate reference); only the threshold moves.

- Centred mean: exact for a linear gradient, but a step at the cell edge gives −17 % or −25 %, so 1.3–1.6 × nominal at α = 0.05 and 3.2–12 × at mission α.
- Past-only mean: a 20 %/m gradient gives up to 33 × at mission α (lane window, 0.1 µSv/h); 5 %/m gives 2.6 ×; a step behind the cell up to 120 ×.
- **Larger of the past and future half-window means:** never biased low by a step or a monotone gradient. Worst 1.03 × at mission α; D# cost ≤ 1.05 (20 %/m on a 5 m lane window), ≤ 1.01 otherwise.

Rule: two adjacent sub-windows, the larger mean, and a stored gradient estimate. One-sided only at a series end, with a gradient guard (below).

## 5. Recounts (task 5)

During a stationary recount, the other three lanes' references count the same field at the same place and time. They are not terms of the m = 1 statistic, so they are independent of it. Ratio-scaled, they give 100 counts in 10 s at 0.1 µSv/h, up to 18 000 at 3 µSv/h, which is more than the predictive requirement in every case (≤ 64 at α = 1.71e-4 for 10 s at 0.1 µSv/h, ≤ 175 at mission α). **No bracketing time is needed**, so there is no change to the search-then-count mission time. Fallback (own lane only, e.g. if a lane ratio is not qualified): bracketing reference time of 14–24 s per recount at 0.1 µSv/h and ≤ 4 s at ≥ 1 µSv/h. That is about +1–8 min per survey for 5–20 expected recounts at 0.1 µSv/h (`results/two_stage.csv` false-recount expectations).

## 6. Search-stage ratemeter (review M2)

The ratemeter must be causal. It should use the same §6 linear statistic over the trailing gates of each lane (the scan dwell, e.g. the last L/v). U_null comes from a past-only lane window: two adjacent past sub-windows, the larger mean, with the predictive threshold at the counts of the sub-window used. It controls **α_scan**, i.e. the expected number of recounts n·α_scan. P_m is controlled at the recount (two-stage rule of §3). Bias toward triggering in a rising field costs recounts, not the false-investigation allowance. Its output is provisional and is never the recorded cell decision.

## 7. `alpha_short` and the beta-window class (review M6)

The model should not take a per-gate `alpha_short` correction as an input. A third stream, with its variance, delivered for about 1 gate in 10, cannot be made exact. Instead:
- Define the beta-window class as "pulses not classified alpha-like by time over threshold".
- Calibrate K, k, b and ε in that definition.
- **Apply the same time-over-threshold cut in the reference chain.** Otherwise bright photon events are removed from F but not from U, and k depends on the photon spectrum and on pile-up rate.
- `alpha_short` becomes a monitored diagnostic: its rate per class is part of the validity envelope, and a gate whose `alpha_short` fraction lies outside the calibrated range is flagged.
- The spectral dependence that remains is carried by u_common(k).

## Proposed wording for MODEL.md

**§3, replace the paragraph "Where U_null comes from (R1)" with:**

> **Where U_null comes from (R1, revision 5).** U_null is estimated from reference counts that are not terms of the statistic under test: by default the lane's own reference gates nearest in time to the dwell, from the complete attachment record, excluding every gate under test of the cell. The window is two adjacent sub-windows (one before and one after the dwell where both exist, otherwise both on the available side), each holding at least N_min reference counts: N_min = 30 for α ≥ 10⁻², 75 for α ≥ 10⁻³, 125 for α ≥ 10⁻⁴, 200 for α ≥ 10⁻⁶ (below that, computed). U_null is the larger of the two sub-window means, and the threshold uses that sub-window's count and live time (below). Where the lane's sub-windows would span more than 2 m of travel or cross a head reset, the four-lane pooled reference over the same rule is used, scaled by the lane's reference-rate ratio estimated over the attachment record, with the effective count 1/(1/N_pool + 1/n_lane + 1/n_all). A reference that includes a gate under test is never used. Stored with every decision: source (lane / pooled), sub-window counts, live times and spans (s, m), the gradient estimate (relative difference of the sub-window means per metre of separation), the lane ratio and its count where pooled. Where both sub-windows lie on one side and the gradient estimate projects a bias of more than 2 % at the dwell, the cell is reported `sensitivity insufficient — reference gradient`. Where fewer than N_min counts exist in the attachment record for every source, it is reported `no decision — null reference unavailable`.
>
> **Threshold with a finite reference.** The threshold is that of the predictive null distribution: P₀(S > t*) averaged over U ~ Gamma(N + ½, scale 1/T) (N, T the count and live time of the U_null sub-window), evaluated with 12 posterior nodes (the average of the characteristic functions in the Gil-Pelaez evaluator). Method string `linear-exact/<evaluator>/predictive-U(N=…)`.
>
> **When a decision is evaluated.** The recorded decision of a cell (D, D*, D#, flag, reason code) is evaluated once, after the cell's U_null sub-windows are complete or at the end of the attachment record, whichever comes first, from all qualifying data and independently of the order in which the gates arrived. Values computed earlier are provisional, are labelled `provisional` wherever they are shown or exported, and may drive the live display and the search stage only. The record keeps, per cell, the time and value of the first provisional alarm and of the recorded decision. A provisional alarm not confirmed by the recorded decision is recorded as `provisional alarm not confirmed`. A recorded alarm without a provisional one is recorded as `alarm found at final evaluation` and enters the residual list for revisit.
>
> **Search stage.** The scan-stage ratemeter that triggers recounts uses the statistic of §6 over the trailing dwell with a past-only U_null under the same rule (two past sub-windows, larger mean, predictive threshold) at α_scan. Its alarms are provisional.
>
> **Recounts.** A stationary recount (m = 1) takes U_null from the other three lanes' reference counts during the recount, scaled by their reference-rate ratios to the tested lane (estimated over the attachment record, uncertainty in the effective count), with the predictive threshold. If a lane ratio is not qualified, the lane's own reference is counted for at least N_min/U before or after the recount, at the same position.

**§6, add after "Cell decision with several lanes":**

> U_null is evaluated per lane under §3; the combined statistic's null distribution uses each lane's predictive U_null (the posterior nodes of the lanes are combined as independent). The stored per-lane fields of §3 are part of the cell record.

**§1, add to the calibration record:** the lane-to-lane reference-rate ratios and their validity (same setup), and the time-over-threshold cut applied in both the floor and the reference chain; `alpha_short` per class is a monitored validity quantity, not an input of §2.

## What remains unvalidated

- N_min values are validated for the beta window with k = 1 and the demonstration calibration terms (g up to 1.73). The alpha channel and other k are not checked here.
- The "larger of two sub-windows" rule is evaluated for bias only. Its effect on precision (each sub-window holds N_min, so the window doubles) is covered by the counts, but it was not simulated end to end.
- Lateral gradients across the head, which bias the pooled and recount references, are not quantified; nor are time-varying fields, the review's pile-up dependence of the time-over-threshold cut, or real lane-ratio stability.
- The probe covers five scenarios of the synthetic simulator, and the expected undecidable fractions come from window counts, not from rerunning the processing with the new rule.
