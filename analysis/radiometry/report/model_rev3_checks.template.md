Generated by `scripts/s7_rev3_checks.py` (run time {{V:rev3_runtime}}). Evidence class: **calculated** (exact lattice convolution of weighted Poisson sums, Gil-Pelaez inversion, saddlepoint) and **simulated** (Monte Carlo, fixed seeds). Every coefficient is the **synthetic** demonstration calibration (`demo_calibration.json`, `field_use_approved: false`) or an assumption of this study; nothing is measured. The verdicts are in `MODEL_REVIEW_rev3.md`; this page holds the numbers.

What is computed. The rule is reproduced exactly as `ros2_ws/src/rr_core/rr_core/estimator.py` evaluates it from the footprint-weighted sums (thresholds agree with that code to 1e-9; `rrrad.rev3.rule_threshold`, `rule_dhash`). The statistic the code actually computes, r = S_F/T_F − k·S_U/T_U − b with S = Σ wᵢNᵢ, is evaluated exactly: weights are kept on a 0.05 lattice (synthetic patterns are exact; geometric weights are rounded to 0.05 and both rule and truth use the rounded values), the pmf of the weighted sum is obtained by FFT of its characteristic function, and the calibration terms are added as the same Gaussian broadening the method assumes (`rrrad.linstat`). U_null is either exact ("U_null exact") or the mean of an independent window of 20·T_F excluding the gates under test, as section 3 requires, averaged over the Poisson distribution of the window count ("window"). Beta window: k = 1, b = 0.5 s⁻¹, u(k) = 0.003 (lane) ⊕ 0.03 (common), u(b) = 0.054 s⁻¹, cov(k,b) = −7.6e-5; "calibration terms none" sets all of them to zero (the case the processing test used). Dose rates are the package's photon-rate equivalents (33.2 s⁻¹ per µSv/h). Mission-level α = {{V:rev3_alpha_sidak}}.

Tolerances. Lattice evaluation: relative error below 1e-4 (unit tests against scipy and Monte Carlo; Gil-Pelaez and lattice agree to 1e-7). U_null window average: quantile-node quadrature, observed deviation from full enumeration {{V:rev3_tol_window_obs}}, tolerance used {{V:rev3_tol_window}}.

### 1.2 Dense scan: the lattice effect of `poisson-exact`

Weights mostly 1 with one small weight, U from 1 to 100 s⁻¹ (0.03–3 µSv/h), T_F = 1, 2.13, 10 s, U_null exact. Worst: **{{V:rev3_A_scan_max}}** ({{V:rev3_A_scan_case}}).

{{T:rev3_A_scan}}

Mechanism. With integer counts the exact threshold is an attainable value of N_F − N_U, and the achieved Poisson probability P(N > n*) sits below α by the discreteness step. The weighted sum takes values between those integers (one count in a 0.1-weight gate adds 0.1), so part of the probability mass that the Poisson model puts exactly on n* lies above it; the convex-order argument controls stop-loss transforms, not this. The effect is largest when nearly all weight is 1 (the variance over-statement is then ≈ 1 and gives no margin) and the counts are small. Spread weights (realistic footprints, all 0.5) are strongly conservative instead.

## 3. One exact linear statistic (candidate rule C)

S = Σᵢ cᵢ rᵢ/Kᵢ = Σⱼ aⱼNⱼ + Gaussian, with cᵢ ∝ 1/Var₀(Dᵢ) (exact null variances, Σw²), Σcᵢ = 1, fixed from U_null before the counts under test are used; floor gates aⱼ = +cᵢwⱼ/(KᵢT_F,ᵢ), reference gates −cᵢkᵢwⱼ/(KᵢT_U,ᵢ); null means (kᵢU₀ + bᵢ)tⱼ and U₀tⱼ; Gaussian variance Σcᵢ²σ²_lane,ᵢ/Kᵢ² + (ΣcᵢU₀u_common,ᵢ/Kᵢ)². One lane, m = 1, is the same statistic.

Where the saddlepoint breaks down: (i) the alpha channel and any statistic whose expected counts are below about 1 with σ_cal ÷ sd below about 0.1 — the distribution is a set of narrow spikes (counts × weights) that the smooth CGF cannot represent, and the Lugannani-Rice value can be off by a factor of several or negative; (ii) any lattice statistic without a Gaussian term whose weights mix 1 with small values (the lattice span is 0.05 but the mass sits near integers), and all-weights-1 statistics below about 30 counts; (iii) near the mean (ŝ → 0; not relevant for decision thresholds). Beta-window statistics with the declared calibration terms and at least about 1 expected floor count: SPA tail error below 0.7 %, achieved/nominal 0.993–1.000. Gil-Pelaez showed no breakdown anywhere in the grid, including 0.008 expected floor counts.
