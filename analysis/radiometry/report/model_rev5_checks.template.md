## 1. One-sided reference (CR-13)

A one-sided reference consists of two adjacent sub-windows before the dwell, B adjacent to it and A beyond, each of N_min counts (N_min from the revision-5 table: 30 at α_scan = 0.05, 75 at 10⁻³, 200 at mission α). At 0.1 µSv/h the source is the pooled window, because the lane window would exceed 2 m; at 1 µSv/h it is the lane window. Speed 0.1 m/s; scan statistic 1 s. Estimators compared:
- rev 5 as written: the larger of the two means;
- rev 5 with its 2 % guard;
- proposed: U_null = max(U_B, 1.5·U_B − 0.5·U_A). This is the local-linear extrapolation to the dwell, never below the near sub-window. When the extrapolation is used, the predictive count is N_eff = 1/(2.25/n_B + 0.25/n_A), so the slope uncertainty widens the posterior.

"Discarded" is the fraction of cells the guard would report `sensitivity insufficient — reference gradient`. The D# ratio is the detection limit at the expected estimate ÷ the limit with U known.
