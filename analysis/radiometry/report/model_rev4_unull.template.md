# The null reference U_null: window in counts, predictive threshold, gradients, recounts

Generated by `scripts/s8_unull.py` (run time {{V:rev4_runtime}}). Evidence class: **calculated** (exact lattice convolution of the decision statistic, summed over the Poisson distribution of the window count) and, for the ros2_ws probe, **simulated** (`scripts/probe_unull_windows.py` on the simulator scenarios; not re-run by `run_all.sh`). All coefficients are synthetic demonstration values; nothing is measured. Verdicts and proposed MODEL.md wording: `../MODEL_REVIEW_rev4.md`.

Statistic: one lane of the revision-4 linear statistic, beta window (k = 1, b = 0.5 s⁻¹, u(k) 0.003 lane ⊕ 0.03 common, u(b) = 0.054 s⁻¹), scan cell 1 s (weights 1; a realistic centred footprint gives the same ratios within 0.02, lower) and stationary recounts of 10, 30 and 60 s. Dose rates as parameterised in the package (33.2 s⁻¹ per µSv/h). Recount α of the demonstration two-stage profile: {{V:rev4_alpha_recount}}; mission-level α 1.14e-5.

## 1. Why cells end as `no decision` in the processing (probe of `rr_core`)

{{T:rev4_probe}}

"Live" is the last evaluation the processor made while the mission ran (the exported value); "final" is one evaluation of every cell at the end with all reference data. Mechanisms, from `rr_core/processing.py` `_u_null`:

1. **Evaluation schedule (implementation).** A cell is re-evaluated only when it gains gates, with the reference data received so far, so the window is truncated at "now" and frozen into the export (independent review C1).
2. **Symmetric window around each gate, per attachment series (implementation).** The window is ±100·T_F around every gate under test, within one attachment's reference series. A head reset starts a new series, and a cell near a series start or end cannot reach 200·T_F even when the other side holds minutes of data.
3. **Requirement in seconds proportional to T_F (rule).** Multi-pass and slow-scan cells have T_F of 1–6 s and need 200–1200 s of reference, whatever the count rate. The same rule accepts edge cells with small T_F on fewer than 50 counts, which is too few for the plug-in threshold at small α.

## 2. What the threshold needs from U_null: counts

The null mean of the statistic is zero for any U (the same-gate reference is subtracted); U_null fixes only the shape of the null distribution. A window of N counts gives U_null a relative error of 1/√N, so the plug-in threshold moves by z·g·δ/2 null standard deviations, where g = d ln Var₀/d ln U (1 for counting-dominated statistics, up to 2 when U²u²(k) dominates). To second order the inflation is exp(z⁴g²/(8N)), so the requirement depends on N, α and g, not on T_F. The exact results:

{{T:rev4_n_ref}}

- **Plug-in** (revision 4): N_ref for ≤ 1.05 at mission α up to {{V:rev4_plug_scan_mis_05}} for scan cells and {{V:rev4_plug_rec_mis_05}} for recounts (≤ 1.10: {{V:rev4_plug_scan_mis_10}} for scans); the formula predicts the exact values to within about 20 %.
- **Predictive threshold** (null distribution averaged over the Jeffreys posterior Gamma(N + ½, T_w) of U; in Gil-Pelaez form, the average of the characteristic functions over 12 posterior nodes): N_ref for ≤ 1.05 at mission α ≤ {{V:rev4_pred_scan_mis_05}} for scans, ≤ {{V:rev4_pred_rec_mis_05}} for recounts (largest case: 60 s at 3 µSv/h, where U²u²(k) dominates and g = 1.7); at α = 0.05, 25 counts (the lower end of the grid) suffice. Over the whole grid, at most {{V:rev4_pred_all_05}} counts for ≤ 1.05.
- **One-sided against centred:** the precision is that of the count; the side does not matter. The two differ only under field variation (section 4).
- **Independence** matters because of correlation, not precision: the same-gate pool fails at any length (revision-3 checks, 21.5× per lane).

### 2.1 Pooled four-lane window: lane response ratio

A pooled window estimates the mean of the four lanes' reference rates. A lane whose reference reads 1 % above the pool is assigned a U_null 1 % low:

{{T:rev4_pooled}}

At mission α: 1 % ratio error {{V:rev4_pool_m1_mis}}, 2 % {{V:rev4_pool_m2_mis}}, 5 % {{V:rev4_pool_m5_mis}}. A pooled window must therefore be scaled by each lane's reference-rate ratio, estimated over the attachment record, and the ratio's statistical uncertainty enters the posterior (effective count 1/(1/N_pool + 1/n_lane,run + 1/n_all,run)). A ratio that differs locally (a lateral field gradient across the 0.26 m head) is a bias this cannot remove; that is the reason to prefer the lane's own window.

### 2.2 Sensitivity cost

{{T:rev4_cost}}

The predictive threshold costs at most {{V:rev4_cost_50_max}} in D# at 50 counts, {{V:rev4_cost_100_max}} at 100 and {{V:rev4_cost_400_max}} at 400. The provably conservative rule (U at the upper 1 − α/10 Poisson bound, threshold at 0.9α) costs up to {{V:rev4_cost_upper_max}} at 100 counts and holds α with a large margin (achieved/nominal 0.03–0.4); it is not recommended.

Including the window count as a term of the statistic (replacing or supplementing the same-gate reference with N_window) also gives a null mean independent of U, and a lower variance. But it compares the floor with a reference taken elsewhere or at another time, which is the uncompensated estimator's weakness. The statistic keeps the same-gate reference, and the window enters only through the threshold.

## 3. Field gradients (scan at 0.1 m/s)

Window of {{V:rev4_NG}} counts (the largest predictive requirement at mission α, all statistics). Length of travel: lane window {{V:rev4_L_lane_01}} m at 0.1 µSv/h and {{V:rev4_L_lane_1}} m at 1 µSv/h; pooled {{V:rev4_L_pool_01}} m and {{V:rev4_L_pool_1}} m. The field rises along travel (worst sign for a past-only window); steps sit at the cell edge in the worst orientation. The statistic itself is unbiased (same-gate reference); only the threshold moves.

{{T:rev4_gradients}}

Worst achieved/nominal at mission α: centred mean {{V:rev4_grad_centred_max}} (steps; {{V:rev4_grad_step_centred}}), past-only mean {{V:rev4_grad_past_max}}, larger of the two half-window means {{V:rev4_grad_max_halves}} (cost in D# at most {{V:rev4_grad_max_halves_dh}} × the unbiased value). A centred mean is exact for a linear gradient but not for a step. The larger of the past and future half-window means is never biased low by a step or a monotone gradient, and costs little.

## 4. Stationary recounts

{{T:rev4_recount}}

During a recount the other three lanes' references count the same field, at the same place and time, and are not part of the m = 1 statistic. With ratio scaling they provide 100–18 000 counts in 10–60 s, more than the predictive threshold needs in every case, so no bracketing time is needed (extra time {{V:rev4_rec_br_max}} s). If only the lane's own reference is accepted, the bracketing time is {{V:rev4_rec_own_min}}–{{V:rev4_rec_own_max}} s per recount (about 14–24 s at 0.1 µSv/h, ≤ 4 s at ≥ 1 µSv/h).
