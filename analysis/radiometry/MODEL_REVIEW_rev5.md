## CR-13: gradient guard (one-sided reference)

**Verdict: the revision-5 guard is wrong and should be removed.** It needs no significance test, because it is the wrong control. With N_min-sized sub-windows it discards 79–90 % of one-sided cells in a uniform field (calculated; the software saw 137 of 232 in s02). Without it, the larger-mean estimator reaches 2.78 × nominal at mission α under a 20 %/m gradient. A significance-tested guard would keep cells, but it would still let through the undetected part of the bias.

**Replacement (validated).** For a one-sided reference with near sub-window B (adjacent to the dwell) and far sub-window A (adjacent beyond it), both of N_min counts:
- U_null = max(U_B, 1.5·U_B − 0.5·U_A): the local-linear extrapolation to the dwell centre, floored at the near sub-window.
- When the extrapolation is used, the predictive count is N_eff = 1/(2.25/n_B + 0.25/n_A), so the slope uncertainty widens the posterior; otherwise it is n_B.
- No cell is discarded. `sensitivity insufficient` arises only through D# > goal, as for any cell.

Calculated at α_scan = 0.05, 10⁻³ and 1.14e-5 (N_min 30, 75, 200), for 0.1 µSv/h (pooled) and 1 µSv/h (lane), 0.1 m/s, and gradients of 0, ±1, ±5, ±12.5 and ±20 %/m plus ×1.5 steps:

| | rev 5 larger mean | rev 5 + 2 % guard | proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| worst achieved/nominal, fields the reference can see | 2.78 | 7.58 (over the few cells kept) | **1.04** |
| D# cost | ≤ 1.17 | — | ≤ **1.08** (≤ 1.03 for gradients) |
| cells discarded | 0 | 79–100 % | **0** |

A step between the near sub-window and the dwell is invisible to any one-sided reference: up to 16 × at mission α and 1.7 × at α_scan, whatever the estimator. One-sided decisions occur only next to an attachment start or end. They are therefore marked in the record, and a cell decided one-sided at an alarm-relevant α should be recounted if flagged.

## CR-14: which α sets N_min

- **Scan cell (m ≥ 1):** a lane's U_null serves the combined test at 0.8α and its own lane test at 0.2α/m. Its N_min is set by the smallest α of the tests that use it, i.e. 0.2α/m. The software's choice is correct.
- **Stationary recount (m = 1):** the combined test and the lane test are the same statistic, so the OR of thresholds at 0.8α and 0.2α is just the 0.8α test. The rule should state that for m = 1 there is a single test at α_recount, which is what controls the mission allowance. N_min = N_min(α_recount) = 125 at the demonstration α_recount = 1.71e-4. The exact predictive requirement is ≤ 100 for 10–60 s recounts at 0.1–3 µSv/h, so 125 has margin.
- **Consequence:** from the other three lanes during the count, 125 counts take 12.6 s at 0.1 µSv/h (2.6 s beyond a 10 s count; none beyond 30 s) and ≤ 1.3 s at ≥ 1 µSv/h. As implemented (200 counts) it is 20 s at 0.1 µSv/h.

## CR-17: search-stage reference after arming

- **Confirmed:** with two sub-windows of N_min(10⁻⁵) = 200 plus 20 pooled counts, the pre-roll is (2·200 + 20)/(4U): 31.6 s at 0.1 µSv/h (26 s at 4 s⁻¹ per lane, as reported), 3.2 s at 1 µSv/h, 1.0 s at 3 µSv/h.
- **Proposed:** the search stage is provisional and controls only the number of recounts, so it needs one past sub-window, not two. Its N_min should come from the scan statistic's predictive requirement (≤ 1.05: 25, 50, 104 counts at α_scan = 0.05, 10⁻³, 10⁻⁵), not from the recount-dominated table. Pooled over four lanes and scaled by the calibration record's lane ratios (the attachment's own ratios do not exist yet), the pre-roll is **7.8 s** at 0.1 µSv/h, 0.8 s at 1 µSv/h and 0.3 s at 3 µSv/h, at α_scan = 10⁻⁵. At α_scan = 0.05 it is 1.9 s at 0.1 µSv/h.
- **Reuse:** reference gates from before a pause or re-arm may be reused when they belong to the same attachment (no head reset), with the same `setup_id` and `cal_id`, the head within 2 m of travel of their position, and taken within 10 min. A pause does not change the detector, and the field is static over that time. Across a head reset (a new attachment) they are not reused.

## CR-15: speed bound of the validity envelope

The envelope's `speed_m_s` is the qualified range of the **measured** gate speed (from the pose interval) over which K and the footprint model were established. It is an upper limit, not a tolerance band. The survey commands a speed at least the speed-control tolerance below it. `demo_calibration.json` now carries `speed_m_s = {min 0, max 0.12}` with that basis. `demo_site_profile_ore.json` carries `scan_speed_m_s.commanded = 0.10`. The software's 2 mm/s numerical tolerance is then unnecessary.

## Multi-lane predictive reference (gap 5)

`rrrad.predictive.PredictiveCombined` is the exact predictive null for m lanes, each with its own posterior. It is the sum over the product of the lanes' 12 posterior nodes, with the common calibration term (Σ gᵢUᵢ)² at every node tuple (12⁴ terms for m = 4, about 1 s). It equals the single-lane rule to 1e-8 and matches a direct Monte Carlo of the predictive null (4·10⁵ draws, two lanes, within 4 SE). Achieved α with windows drawn and the truth at the true U is 1.03 ± 0.03 (α = 10⁻³) and 1.03 ± 0.06 (mission), for m = 2 with 100 counts per lane.

The software's form, with the cross terms 2Σ_{i<j} gᵢgⱼU0ᵢU0ⱼ at point values, is slightly anti-conservative: the exact tail at its threshold is up to 1.0023 × α for m = 2 and **1.035 × α** for m = 4 (33 s⁻¹, 200 counts, mission α). **Recommendation:** use the exact node product. Test vectors: `results/rev5_predictive_vectors.json`. Cases m = 1, 2, 4 at U = 5 and 33.2 s⁻¹, α = 0.05, 10⁻³ and 1.14e-5, each with t* exact, t* with the approximation, and D# for m ≤ 2. Agreement tolerance: t* relative 1e-6 for the exact form.

## Proposed wording for MODEL.md (revision 6)

**§3, in "Where U_null comes from", replace the sentence beginning "Where both sub-windows lie on one side …" with:**

> Where both sub-windows lie on one side of the dwell (near sub-window B adjacent to the dwell, far sub-window A beyond it), U_null = max(U_B, 1.5·U_B − 0.5·U_A), the local-linear extrapolation to the dwell floored at the near sub-window. When the extrapolation is used, the predictive count is N_eff = 1/(2.25/n_B + 0.25/n_A); otherwise it is n_B. No cell is discarded for a gradient. The decision is marked `one-sided reference`, and the gradient estimate is stored. A step between the near sub-window and the dwell cannot be detected from one side; a one-sided cell that flags is recounted before it is reported as an investigation.

**§3, add after the N_min table:**

> N_min is set by the smallest α of the tests that use that U_null: 0.2α/m for the lanes of a scan cell. For m = 1 (stationary recount) the combined and the lane test are the same statistic, and the cell is one test at α (α_recount for a recount).

**§3, replace the paragraph "Search stage" with:**

> **Search stage.** The scan-stage ratemeter uses the statistic of §6 over the trailing dwell with a past-only U_null of one sub-window. That sub-window holds at least N_scan(α_scan) counts (25 for α_scan ≥ 10⁻², 50 for ≥ 10⁻³, 105 for ≥ 10⁻⁵) from the four-lane pool, scaled by the calibration record's lane ratios, with the predictive threshold. Its alarms are provisional and control only the number of recounts. Reference gates before a pause or re-arm are reused when they belong to the same attachment, `setup_id` and `cal_id`, lie within 2 m of travel, and are less than 10 min old. After arming or a head reset, the ratemeter does not alarm until its sub-window is complete. The pre-roll time is N_scan/(4U): 7.8 s at 0.1 µSv/h for α_scan = 10⁻⁵.

**§6, add to "Cell decision with several lanes":**

> The predictive null of the combined statistic is evaluated exactly over the product of the lanes' posterior nodes, with the common calibration term (Σᵢ cᵢU_i u_common,i(k)/Kᵢ)² at each node tuple. Evaluating its cross terms at point values exceeds α by up to 3.5 % for four lanes and is not used.

**§1, add to the validity envelope:**

> `speed_m_s` is the qualified range of the measured gate speed. Surveys command a speed at least the speed-control tolerance below its maximum. The record's `identity` member {head_serial, electronics_revision, firmware, setup_id, cal_id} and its schema are those of `analysis/radiometry/schemas/calibration_record.schema.json`.

**§9, replace "Not yet validated: the alpha channel and k ≠ 1 under the predictive threshold" with:**

> The alpha channel (demonstration coefficients) and k = 0.3 under the predictive threshold: achieved/nominal ≤ 1.054 and ≤ 1.023 at the N_min table (calculated).

## What remains unvalidated

- The one-sided estimator was checked for linear gradients and single steps with a stationary field. Curved fields, time-varying fields, and the interaction with the pooled lane ratio were not checked.
- Undetectable steps next to the dwell remain a risk of one-sided decisions; only the recount bounds them.
- The search-stage pre-roll uses calibration-record lane ratios whose real stability is unknown.
- Reuse limits for paused data (10 min, 2 m) are judgment, not calculation.
- The multi-lane achieved-α check is Monte Carlo with 80 window draws, m = 2 only (SE 3–6 %). For m = 4, only the evaluator is cross-checked, not the achieved α.
- Alpha channel at 30 s and 1 µSv/h, and k = 0.3 at 1 µSv/h, are computed only with `RRRAD_FULL=1`.
- Nothing has been compared with detector data.
