# Review of `MODEL.md` revision 3 (section 6, IB-31) by the radiometry analyst

Scope: the two revision-3 rules of `MODEL.md` §6 ("Weighted sums", "Cell decision with several lanes"), the N+1 rule and the 0.10 minimum lane-gate weight, and the candidate single linear statistic (task C from the lead). `MODEL.md` was not edited. All numbers are **calculated** (exact lattice convolution, Gil-Pelaez inversion) or **simulated** (Monte Carlo, fixed seeds) with the **synthetic** demonstration coefficients; no coefficient is measured. Evidence: `report/model_rev3_checks.md` (generated by `scripts/s7_rev3_checks.py`, run by `run_all.sh`), `results/rev3_*.csv`, code `rrrad/linstat.py`, `rrrad/rev3.py`, tests `tests/test_rev3.py`. The rule is reproduced as `ros2_ws/src/rr_core/rr_core/estimator.py` computes it (thresholds equal to 1e-9). Tolerances: exact evaluations to 1e-4 relative or better; U_null-window averages to 2 %.

## Verdicts

## (A) Weighted sums

**`poisson-exact` is not conservative.** Achieved ÷ nominal false-alarm probability, one lane, clean cell, U_null exact (no window noise), method as §3 selects:

- Beta window, the processing engineer's case (weights 1 × 7 + 0.1, U = 3 s⁻¹, 0.3 s gates, no calibration terms): exact **1.160 (m = 2), 1.239 (m = 4)**, which confirms the ros2_ws Monte Carlo (1.16, 1.24).
- Beta window, dense scan U = 1–100 s⁻¹, T_F = 1–10 s, weights mostly 1 with one small weight: up to **1.24** with the declared calibration terms (3 full + one 0.1, α = 1e-3, U = 1.2 s⁻¹) and **1.66** without them. On the survey grid of the report (0.1/1/3 µSv/h, 1 s and 10 s), worst 1.11.
- Alpha channel (k ≈ 0.005, 0.02–5 expected floor counts): up to **4.41** (all weights 0.10, 0.1 µSv/h, 1 s, α = 0.05), 1.19 (7 full + one 0.1, 1 µSv/h, 1 s, α = 1e-3), 1.04 for a realistic centred-lane footprint.

Mechanism: the exact threshold sits on an attainable integer value of N_F − N_U, so the Poisson model's achieved probability is below α by the discreteness step; the weighted sum takes values between those integers and fills the step. In the alpha channel the threshold is "any count", whose probability follows the raw mean Σλ of the kept gates while the model uses the weighted mean Σwλ, up to 1/w_min = 10 times smaller. Convex order bounds stop-loss transforms, not tail probabilities at a lattice threshold; the text's own caveat is borne out.

**Where (A) holds.** Spread weights are strongly conservative: realistic footprints of a 100 cm² cell (80 mm lanes, 100 mm apertures eroded by 10 mm, 0.1 m/s, 0.1 s gates) give achieved/nominal 0.09–0.69 at α = 0.05 and below 0.13 at α = 1.14e-5 in the beta window. `normal` where §3 selects it: worst 1.01 for weighted patterns, and the same cases with all weights 1 give 1.08–1.16 at α = 1e-3 just above the 30-count switch — an R2 residual, not a weighting effect. The true detection limit of the Poisson-form threshold is never above the quoted one (ratio ≤ 1.00).

**Price.** Quoted r# ÷ exact-distribution r#: realistic footprints 1.11–1.67 (cell centred on a seam 1.44–1.67), all weights 0.5: 1.34–1.53. The two refinements named in the text are not valid tests either: the moment-matched scaled Poisson (N_eff) reaches achieved/nominal **1.33**, the exact-variance normal form 4.70 at low counts.

**U_null window (a §3 finding, not a weighting effect).** With U_null from a 20·T_F window, even all-weights-1 `poisson-exact` reaches **1.41 × nominal** at α = 1.14e-5 (0.1 µSv/h, 1 s); the combined statistic of (C) 1.91 (m = 2, seam cell). With 200·T_F: 1.04 and 1.07; with 1000·T_F: 1.01.

## (B) Any lane at α/m

- **False alarm.** With realistic footprints, m = 2 and 4, common term u_common(k) integrated exactly: worst cell achieved/nominal 0.108 (very conservative because of (A)). The union-bound argument is arithmetic and correct, but only on top of per-lane tests that hold α/m: every per-lane excess of (A) passes straight to the cell (1.160, 1.239 above). The **pooled four-lane U_null fallback over the same gates** contains the tested lane's own reference counts and re-creates part of the R1 coupling: per-lane achieved/nominal **21.5** (m = 4, mission α, 0.1 µSv/h, 1 s), 12.7 for m = 1, 1.71 at α = 0.05; Monte Carlo of four lanes sharing the pooled U_null and the common term: cell **1.55 ± 0.01** at α = 0.05. Positive correlation lowers P(any) below the union bound by less than 1 % in these cases.
- **Detection at the quoted limit.** Activity extending beyond the cell (footprints fully covered): P(detect) at minᵢ D#ᵢ > 0.999 — holds. Activity over one lane's footprint, at that lane's D#ᵢ: ≥ 0.991 — holds. **Activity confined to the cell**: each gate sees only the fraction wᵢ, so the footprint-average activity is Σw²t/Σwt of the cell's; P(detect) at the quoted limit 0.03–0.91, true limit up to **1.81 ×** the quoted one. The sentence "for activity uniform over the cell every contributing lane sees the same D" is false unless the activity extends at least one footprint beyond the cell. **Seam patch** (100 cm² square centred on a seam): P(detect) at maxᵢ D#ᵢ 0.04–0.82, true limit up to **1.77 ×** maxᵢ D#ᵢ.
- **Sensitivity cost** (activity over the footprints, large-count regime where a normal inverse-variance combination is valid): any lane at α/m ÷ normal combination with the same variances **1.32–1.57 (m = 2), 1.61–2.38 (m = 4)**; ÷ the exact combined statistic of (C) up to 2.41 (m = 2) and 3.58 (m = 4). The processing report (median 0.179 → 0.304 Bq/cm² in s01) is consistent: on an s01-like plan (two passes offset by half a pitch, 0.03 m/s, U = 5 s⁻¹, m = 2–3) the medians are 0.190 (rev 2), 0.252 (rev 3), 0.143 (C), from approximate geometry.

## N+1 and the 0.10 minimum weight (task 3)

Alpha channel, clean cell, exact: E[reported u_stat²] ÷ true counting variance 1.0–322 (6.1 at 1 µSv/h, 1 s, weights 1; 1.1 at 10 s); coverage of r ± 2u_stat ≥ 0.945. The N+1 rule does not bias the reported uncertainty downward; it over-states it at low counts. It does not touch the decision (thresholds use U_null). One inconsistency: N+1 applies only at S_F = 0, so one count in a 0.1-weight gate reports u_stat = 0.32/T_F, smaller than for no count (1/T_F); P(0 < S_F < 1) reaches 0.3–0.97 for partial footprints. The 0.10 weight floor does affect the decision, through (A): the alpha-channel failures above (up to 4.41) come from cells built from low-weight gates.

## (C) One exact linear statistic

Definition: S = Σᵢ cᵢrᵢ/Kᵢ = Σⱼ aⱼNⱼ + Z with floor coefficients +cᵢwⱼ/(KᵢT_F,ᵢ), reference coefficients −cᵢkᵢwⱼ/(KᵢT_U,ᵢ), cᵢ ∝ 1/Var₀(Dᵢ) from the exact null variances (Σw²) at U_null, Σcᵢ = 1; null means (kᵢU₀ + bᵢ)tⱼ, U₀tⱼ; Z Gaussian with variance Σcᵢ²σ²_lane,ᵢ/Kᵢ² + (ΣcᵢU₀u_common,ᵢ/Kᵢ)². CGF closed form.

- **Gil-Pelaez** (midpoint rule, step set by the support width, cut-off from the Gaussian factor): worst relative tail error **5.7e-8** against exact lattice or outer-sum references, over one lane, two lanes combined and the alpha channel, 0.008–590 expected floor counts, α down to 1.14e-5; achieved/nominal 1.000000. Four lanes: Monte Carlo (10⁶) within 1.4 standard errors. It needs σ_cal > 0, which u(b) > 0 guarantees; without a Gaussian term exact lattice convolution is used (weights on a 0.05 lattice).
- **Saddlepoint (Lugannani-Rice)**: beta window with the declared calibration terms and ≥ 1 expected floor count: tail error < 0.7 %, achieved/nominal 0.993–1.000. **Breaks down** in the alpha channel and wherever expected counts are below about 1 with σ_cal/sd below about 0.1 (spiky distribution; errors by factors of several, negative values; achieved/nominal 0.17–2.09), and in its lattice form for mixed weights or few counts without a Gaussian term (achieved/nominal up to 1.71). It is not acceptable as the decision evaluator; it may seed the root search.
- **Detection limit** at (α, β): the D at which P(S > t* | D) = 1 − β, the alternative adding Kᵢ·D·fⱼtⱼ to the floor means (fⱼ = footprint fraction over the activity, 1 for activity covering the footprints) and u_rel(K)·E[S − S₀] as a Gaussian term.
- **Single-lane patches are diluted** by cᵢ ≈ 1/m: on the s01-like plan the worst-lane D# for activity over one lane's footprint is 0.429 Bq/cm² with C alone, 0.441 for rev 3. A parallel exact per-lane test at a split α protects them at small cost: C at 0.8α plus per-lane at 0.2α/m gives medians **0.148** (uniform) and **0.311** (one lane); 0.5/0.5 gives 0.158 and 0.286. Recommended split: 0.8 / 0.2.
- **Window**: C is exact at U₀ but inherits the U_null window effect (1.91 at 20·T_F, 1.07 at 200·T_F, mission α).

**Recommendation for revision 4:** rule C with the Gil-Pelaez evaluator (lattice convolution when σ_cal = 0), a parallel per-lane exact test at 0.2α/m, U_null from a window of at least 200·T_F excluding the gates under test, no same-gate pooled fallback.

## Proposed wording for MODEL.md

**§3, replace the paragraph "Where U_null comes from (R1)" from "by default …" to the end with:**

> By default U_null is the mean reference rate of the lane over a window of at least 200·T_F centred on the dwell **excluding the gates under test** (20·T_F is sufficient only for per-decision α ≥ 0.01). Where the lane window is shorter, the four-lane pooled reference rate over a window of the same length, also excluding the gates under test, is used. A reference that includes the gates under test (for example the four-lane pool over the same gates) is never used for U_null; where no qualifying window exists the cell is reported `no decision — null reference unavailable`. The method and the window length (s, and reference counts) are stored with every decision.

**§3, replace the paragraph "Thresholds at small α (R2)" with:**

> **Thresholds (R2, revision 4).** Every decision statistic is a fixed linear combination of independent Poisson gate counts plus a Gaussian calibration term, S = Σⱼ aⱼNⱼ + Z (§6). The threshold t* is the value with P₀(S > t*) = α under the null means computed from U_null, evaluated from the exact distribution: by numerical inversion of the characteristic function (Gil-Pelaez), or by lattice convolution when all aⱼ are integer multiples of one span (always when Z = 0). Saddlepoint and normal approximations may seed the root search but are not used for the decision. The detection limit is the activity D at which P(S > t* | D) = 1 − β, with the signal added to the floor means and u_rel(K) as a Gaussian term proportional to the signal. The stored method is `linear-exact/gil-pelaez` or `linear-exact/lattice`; a numerical check (the tail at t* recomputed with half the quadrature step must agree to 10⁻³ relative) is stored, and a cell failing it is reported `no decision — evaluator check failed`.

**§6, replace the paragraph "Weighted sums (IB-31, first part)" with:**

> **Weighted sums.** Footprint weights enter the decision statistic as coefficients of the individual gate counts: a floor count of lane i in gate j has coefficient wⱼ/T_F,ᵢ and its variance contribution wⱼ²λⱼ. The revision-3 treatment (Poisson form evaluated with weighted sums) is withdrawn: with `poisson-exact` it exceeds α by up to 1.24 in the beta window and 4.4 in the alpha channel (calculated, synthetic coefficients, `analysis/radiometry/MODEL_REVIEW_rev3.md`), and where it is conservative it costs 1.1–1.7 in r#. The moment-matched scaled-Poisson form is not used either (it exceeds α by up to 1.33). The export manifest records `weighted_sum_variance: exact_coefficients`. Rates remain footprint averages: activity confined to a cell is reported, and detected, as its footprint average, which for a cell traversed by partial footprints is Σw²t/Σwt of the cell's activity (0.44–0.74 for the 0.4 geometry: lane centred 0.74, cell centred on a seam 0.44).

**§6, replace the paragraph "Cell decision with several lanes (IB-31, second part)" and its list with:**

> **Cell decision with several lanes.** For the m contributing lanes (decision weight ≥ 0.10) the cell statistic is S = Σᵢ cᵢ rᵢ/Kᵢ, with cᵢ proportional to the inverse null variance of Dᵢ (exact coefficients, evaluated at U_null before the counts under test are used) and Σcᵢ = 1; the common calibration terms enter Z fully correlated across lanes: Var(Z) = Σcᵢ²σ²_lane,ᵢ/Kᵢ² + (Σcᵢ U_null,ᵢ u_common,ᵢ(k)/Kᵢ)². The cell is flagged when S exceeds its threshold at 0.8α **or** any lane's own statistic (the same construction with m = 1) exceeds its threshold at 0.2α/m; by the union bound the cell false-alarm probability is at most α. The quoted cell D# is that of the combined test for activity covering every contributing footprint. A patch claim uses the detection limit of the same rule with the patch's footprint fractions in the alternative, at the least favourable alignment (straddling a lane seam and a cell boundary); maxᵢ D#ᵢ of the lanes is not a patch limit. The reported D, u_stat and u_total are the same combination (estimate), with the common terms added after combination. Stored per cell: m, cᵢ, U_null and its window per lane, t* and the evaluator of the combined test, the per-lane thresholds, the α split (0.8/0.2), which test(s) flagged, and the rule string `linear-exact/combined+per-lane/0.8-0.2`. A stationary recount is the same rule with m = 1.

**§2, low counts, replace the sentence on N+1 with (minor):**

> Low counts: the reported counting variance of a weighted sum is max(Σw²N, Σw²λ₀), with λ₀ the null means at U_null (not N+1); a zero-count result is never presented as certainty.

## Limits of this check

Top-hat footprint response; weights rounded to 0.05 (rule and statistic both use the rounded weights); k set to 1 in the beta window (the demo k values are within 3 %); U_null window modelled as a Poisson count of a stationary field (field gradients over a 200·T_F window are not modelled and bias U_null, a reason to prefer the four-lane pooled window); the s01-like geometry approximates the plan; D# probabilities in (B) are checked with u_rel(K) = 0.
