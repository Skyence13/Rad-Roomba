- [ ] Immobilize the trailer under site procedure. Define the survey boundary, threshold, edges, parking and recovery points. - [ ] Confirm drive inhibited before lifting. Check integral grip, battery latch, charge inlet cap and unplugged charger. Inspect tires, caster, head mount, connector retention, low bumper, skids/grille and entrance window. Check for carryover contamination.
- [ ] Verify expected **head type, serial and geometry on the host**. Test contact bumpers and local stops separately on the approved fixture. Look for drift, window damage or irregular standoff.
- [ ] For browser operation, log in locally from a phone or computer. Confirm the robot identity, controlling user and read-only observers. Compare local indications for ready/hold, stop and charger/service status with the web page. Confirm the ordinary program Pause/Stop and separate latching E-stop. - [ ] Confirm matching mission mode. ## Survey and holds

- [ ] Check the trailer frame, initial position and heading (the robot’s **pose**), planned lanes and permitted margins. Local monitoring records pose quality, coverage, counts and reference live time, temperatures, reserve and protective inputs throughout the run.
- [ ] A commissioning run holds if the required supervisor link is lost. In approved unattended mode an ordinary viewer closing the page or dropping off Wi-Fi may leave the local survey running. In shared space, an attendant outside the protected envelope may remain during motion. Intrusion or loss of local protection invokes a local stop. Opening the protected access boundary stops the secured-empty mode. Poor localization, loss of valid acquisition or low reserve invokes the specified mission hold independently of web connectivity. Retain the event and affected floor area. Follow the prescribed response to failed alert delivery.
- [ ] Record areas needing slow rescan or stationary count. If boots, hand instruments or other obstructions mask a planned footprint, withhold coverage, hold or defer the leg and resurvey the unobstructed area. A position-uncertain leg also remains unresolved until recovered. Keep unmeasured strips, poor sensitivity, uncertain position and offset/wall constraints visible. Before putting hands, tools or feet within reach of the head, wheels or moving path, use the hands-on inhibit and independently confirm stopped traction. Apply the site doorway/edge and retrieval procedure.

## Guided hotspot tour

- [ ] Load the selected run and check the persistent hotspot IDs, original marks, uncertainty, trailer orientation, parking location and proposed visit order. Claim command control. Check the intended travel and turning envelope for the selected mode. Attendants can stay elsewhere in the trailer throughout shared-space follow-up.
- [ ] At **Arrived**, verify the identified detector lane aligned to the hotspot and the displayed residual position error. The robot remains parked until an accepted command. A separate accessible offset or retreat may be needed to uncover the spot for a hand probe, wipe or marking. Use cooperative coordinate marking from the known floor datum.
- [ ] Use the hands-on inhibit and verify stopped traction for close inspection or handling. Record findings against the hotspot ID. **Next**, **Expose spot** and **Park** may command motion with attendants inside the trailer when the protected envelope is clear and the hands-on inhibit is released. After an intrusion or hands-on hold, clear people and tools from the local movement envelope, deliberately rearm, then issue a fresh command. A command rejected while inhibited stays rejected. Defer unreachable or ambiguous points with a reason. Verify arrival at the final safe parking location.

## Recovery, battery and disposition

- [ ] Stop, inhibit and secure drives before retrieval. Handle the head window, wheels, skids and tray under contamination control. Recheck response/window integrity after contact or damage and qualify affected data using the site procedure.
- [ ] For a pack swap, park, checkpoint/close the run and isolate load before unlatching the pack. Cap and segregate removed packs as prescribed. Check replacement pack temperature, identity/state of charge, engagement and latch. Restore power deliberately and verify health, clock/pose and safe mode; resurvey gaps if necessary.
- [ ] Charge using the approved charger/pack and eligible cell temperature. Verify the charge-present hardware drive inhibit. In limited charge/service mode, use only authorized settings and data/export functions. Disconnect and rearm deliberately before any later mission.
- [ ] Review raw counts, live times, time/pose quality, immutable original flags and follow-up history. Reconcile gaps and minimum detectable activity with the selected procedure. Record any supplemental survey, wipes and final site RP decision.
