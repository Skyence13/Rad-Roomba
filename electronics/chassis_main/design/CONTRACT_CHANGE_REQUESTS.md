# Chassis electronics: contract change requests and deviations (stage 1)

Owner: chassis electronics. Addressed to the integration lead unless another owner is named. Nothing here has been applied to `interface/system/contract.yaml`.
"Deviation" = the design already differs and needs a decision; "request" = a change wanted from another owner; "note" = information the other owner needs.

| ID | Type | To | Subject | What and why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCR-E01 | deviation | integration | `power.rails.VBAT_SW` "after main ideal-diode/eFuse" | The pack switch is a reverse-polarity-protected **bidirectional** MOSFET pair (LM74502), not an ideal diode and not an eFuse. An ideal diode would block the on-board charger (which charges through VBAT_SW) and all regeneration. Consequences: no electronic current limit at pack level (20 A fuse + BMS + branch eFuses protect); regenerated energy returns to the pack when the BMS accepts it, the hardware clamp at 15.6 V takes it when it does not. Proposed wording: "after the main reverse-polarity switch (bidirectional, selector-enabled, over-voltage cut-off)". |
| CCR-E02 | request | chassis firmware | Read IOX_B P17 as input `CHAIN_END_RB` | The pin map has no readback of the chain node after the ARM_PERMIT element, so a shorted ARM_PERMIT photo-MOS or a stuck PB10 driver cannot be seen. The node tap is wired to IOX_B P17 (listed as SPARE_OUT). Read it (I²C, slow is fine) and compare with the commanded ARM_PERMIT at boot and at every change; never drive P17. |
| CCR-E03 | note | chassis firmware | Electrical constants behind the adopted pin map | (a) VREF+ is a 3.000 V ±0.2 % reference (REF3030), not 3.3 V: all ADC scaling and the NTC/PRES_ID pull-ups use 3.000 V. (b) IPROPI: 2.49 kΩ → 1.1205 V/A, i.e. `drive.ipropi_a_per_v` = 0.8925; the bridges chop in hardware at 2.68 A (full scale), so the "3.0 V at 6.5 A" scaling in the pin map note is not what is fitted. Reason: 6.5 A would be above the DRV8874's 6 A minimum over-current threshold and gives no hardware limit on stall thrust. (c) HEAD_PRES_ID has no series resistance in the measured path (4.70 kΩ pull-up only). (d) Chain readbacks are node voltages: "live up to and including this element"; an element behind an open one must be reported unknown. (e) BRAKE_x_RB is an OR over both poles and valid only while the bridges are asleep. (f) GPA5/6/7 are comparator outputs as requested; the photo-MOS contacts behind them are inferred from BODY_BUMPER_L_RB. (g) ToF GPIO1 lines end on test pads. (h) No MCU pin exists for pack presence or BUS_5V fault (test points only). |
| CCR-E04 | deviation | integration, mechanical | RR-PANEL "button/LED harness landing" | The REARM, PAUSE, HANDS-ON, E-stop and selector devices land on RR-CHS-MAIN (J_BTN, J_HANDS, J_ESTOP, J_MODE), not on RR-PANEL, so the hardwired set path and chain do not pass through the panel board and its cable. RR-PANEL carries the five LEDs and the sounder only. |
| CCR-E05 | request | integration, EDA (RR-TOF) | Signal connector temperature rating | JST GH (fixed for RR-TOF by the task and by RR-TOF-A) is recalled as −25…+85 °C [U]: no margin at the −25 °C objective and it does not meet the "−40 °C-rated families" instruction. All power and safety connectors are −40 °C families. Either accept GH inside the heated shell and verify the perimeter ToF positions in the cold trial, or move RR-TOF and the main-board signal connectors to a −40 °C 1.25 mm family (Molex Micro-Lock Plus) in rev B. |
| CCR-E06 | note | integration | Branch limits as built | VMOT electronic limit 6.5 A (contract ceiling 12 A); each bridge chops at 2.68 A. V5_CM5 5.0 A (eFuse on the 5 V side; the rail is 5.12 V nominal). HEAD_HTR 4.05 A (3.6–4.4 A) against the M16 contact's 5 A. BUS_5V ≈ 0.17 A typ [U]. Charger 3.0 A, input limit 4.0 A [U]. |
| CCR-E07 | request | harness, head electronics | LAB extension conductor size | The head ICD's 0.125 Ω added-path budget is met at 2 m only with ≥ 0.75 mm² (AWG 18) heater cores: 94 mΩ [C]. AWG 20 gives 143 mΩ (15 % over), a moulded AWG 24 cordset 347 mΩ. Confirm that the M16 8-pole contacts accept 0.75 mm²; if not, shorten the extension to 1.7 m or relax the budget to 0.15 Ω (`electronics/analysis/power_budget.md` §6). |
| CCR-E08 | request | mechanical | Board-level fit items | (a) Pack feed and charge-inlet feed share **one** 8-way Mini-Fit Jr header (22.2 mm) on the right edge rear, because two separate power connectors do not fit the 26.5 mm left between H3's keep-out and v = 34; the pack harness therefore needs a 2-into-1 loom to the coupler and the inlet. (b) Tallest parts: brake relays 15.7 mm, Mini-Fit header 12.8 mm, six 10.5 mm capacitors: all kept out of the 12 mm front-corner zones. (c) Most small connectors are low-profile **top-entry** headers (4.3–6 mm, cable leaves sideways below 12 mm) placed inboard of the named edges, because the allocated edge lengths (282 mm) cannot take 35 connectors side by side. Confirm 12 mm of clear height over the rear corners and the front-centre strip. (d) RR-PANEL: two M2.5 holes are proposed at (3.5, 20.5) and (66.5, 20.5) mm; the bottom-side GH headers are 4.25 mm against the 3 mm bottom limit: allow 4.5 mm locally or say so and they move to the top side. (e) The bimetal cut-out and the 20 W / 7.2 Ω element of the bay heater are in the heater harness, not on the board. |
| CCR-E09 | note | integration, documentation | Charge / OFF behaviour to put in the operator text | Charging needs the selector in SERVICE (or RUN) and a pack fitted; in OFF a connected charger does nothing. Service on the charger without a pack is not supported. OFF is a MOSFET switch-off, not isolation: isolate by removing the pack and the charge plug. Below +5 °C at the pack-surface NTC charging is held off in hardware; the bay heater then runs from the pack (it is on the battery side), so a cold, empty pack is warmed at the expense of its own charge before charging starts. |
| CCR-E10 | request | mechanical, robot software | USB ports on the selected carrier | The design uses two USB cables to the CM5 (MCU CDC link and the FT230X head-link bridge; no hub, so one link cannot take the other down). The Waveshare CM5-IO-BASE-A has two USB-A ports: both are then used and the USB-Ethernet service adapter the mechanical lead mentions has no port unless the carrier's two further USB 2.0 ports (FFC) are brought out [U]. Decide: break out the FFC ports, or accept Wi-Fi-only service, or choose a two-RJ45 carrier. |
| CCR-E11 | note | chassis firmware | Watchdog output to NRST | Implemented (Schottky + solder jumper JP1, normally closed). With JP1 closed the watchdog resets the MCU every ~230 ms whenever it is not strobed: SWD halts and the ROM bootloader (DFU) only work with JP1 open, and the firmware must give its first strobe within 23 ms of reset release [D TPS3430 tWDU min]. If the boot path cannot guarantee that, leave JP1 open and rely on the MCU's own IWDG for the reset; the chain element works either way. |
| CCR-E12 | note | integration | Owner decision "Pause may be cleared remotely" | No hardware impact: PAUSE is a contact to IOX_A (program control) and is not in the chain. E-stop, HANDS-ON and REARM remain hardwired and local. |
