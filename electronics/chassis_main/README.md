## Commands

```
python3 electronics/chassis_main/design/circuit.py          # stage-1 self-check (baseline)
python3 electronics/chassis_main/overrides.py               # stage-2 change log + self-check on the overridden netlist
python3 tools/eda/rrkicad.py sch   electronics/chassis_main/board.py              # schematic, netlist cross-check, ERC, PDF (~10 s)
python3 tools/eda/rrkicad.py build electronics/chassis_main/board.py --no-route   # + placed PCB, fan-out, escape, DRC, outputs (~2 min)
RR_FREEROUTING_JAVA_OPTS=-Xmx6g python3 tools/eda/rrkicad.py build electronics/chassis_main/board.py   # + autorouter (route/ SES cached)
tools/docker/kicad.sh python3 electronics/chassis_main/tools/fitcheck.py         # courtyard area per side, zone checks -> reports/fitcheck.json
python3 electronics/chassis_main/tools/cable_lengths.py                          # harness length re-estimate -> reports/cable_lengths.json
python3 electronics/chassis_main/tools/near_report.py 10                         # passives farther than 10 mm from their anchor pin
python3 electronics/chassis_main/tools/freeze_auto.py                            # freeze auto-arranged parts after a build
```
Delete `placement_auto.py` to let the auto-arranger re-place the passives (then freeze again).

## Frame mapping

`mechanical/common/board_envelopes.yaml` RR-CHS-MAIN (u, v) → tool board frame **(X, Y) = (u, v)**, no rotation, no offset:
origin = rear-left corner seen from above, X = u towards the robot's right (base_link −y), Y = v forward (base_link +x), top side up.
base_link = (−67 + Y, 80 − X, 83 + z) mm (board top at z = 84.6). Holes H1..H6 at the envelope (u, v); IMU U42 centred at (80, 67);
THERMAL_TO_BRIDGE pad u 50–110, v 30–70; front corners u < 14 / u > 146, v > 88. The pick-and-place file, `out/pinmap.json`, the STEP
models and the drill origin all use this frame.

## Copper

* In1.Cu: solid GND. In4.Cu splits (non-overlapping): 3V3 (logic, u 0.5–50, v 27–62), VMOT (u 58–102, v 37–62), VSYS (charger,
  u 104–159.5, v 42–60), VBAT_SW (rear band and right side, and the front-left head-branch area). rrkicad's plane fan-out ties SMD
  pads of those nets to their plane.
* Net classes: HC_PACK 1.0 mm, HC_VMOT / HC_BRANCH 0.8 mm, PWR_1A5 0.4 mm, SAFETY_CHAIN 0.4 mm / 0.2 mm clearance, signals 0.2 mm.
  These are the autorouter widths; the guidance widths for 20 A / 6.5 A / 5 A (12.3 / 2.6 / 1.8 mm) need pours or hand-drawn
  copper that is **not yet done** (open item).
* Keep-outs: 7 mm around every mounting hole (vias, parts), no vias under the IMU, no bottom parts on the gap pad.

## Clearances

KiCad applies a net-class clearance also between pads of one footprint, and rrkicad's plane fan-out places a via with its own net's
clearance (not its neighbour's). A 0.5 mm SAFETY_CHAIN clearance therefore produced DRC errors at fine-pitch pins and next to fan-out
vias; the DRC class clearance is 0.2 mm. The guidance's functional clearances (0.5 mm chain-to-anything, 1.0 mm chain-to-VBAT_SW/
VMOT/VSYS/V12_AUX, 1.0 mm between adjacent chain nodes, 2 mm loop-sense to heater copper) are a **manual review item** until the tool
supports custom DRC rules.

How the post stage was run: the build's own `_post` crashed in the tool's new unused-escape clean-up
(`pcb.py _remove_unused_escapes`: `TypeError: 'SwigPyObject' object is not iterable`; pcb.py was being edited during the run) and
the host then started the finisher on an un-imported board (1165 "open"), which was stopped. The SES was imported with
`tools/docker/kicad.sh python3 tools/eda/rrkicad.py _post electronics/chassis_main/board.py` after moving
`route/escape_vias.json` aside (that file only drives the clean-up), so unused escape vias remain (25 via_dangling warnings).
A plain `build` re-uses the cached SES but will hit the same crash until the tool is fixed.

Judgement — what it takes to finish: the router is CPU-starved and far from done after one pass (31 % of the items routed in
13 min); STRESS-150 needed ~20-40 passes. Realistic routes to completion, in order of effect: (1) hand-drawn copper for the power
nets that dominate the open list (pours/pre-routes for VBAT_RAW/PSW_SRC/VBAT_SW at the power entry, VMOT, VSYS, V5_BUCK/V5_CM5,
heater branches) and extend the In4 splits / add In3 pours for V5_AUX and VREF_3V0; (2) fix the ~70 passives placed > 20 mm from
their pins and the dense rear-left corner; (3) then several continue runs of 20-30 min each on an otherwise idle machine plus the
finisher once the tool's import is fixed; (4) whatever remains (expect tens of connections) by hand in KiCad. The safety-chain group
routing and the critical-net pre-routes of guidance §6 are not done and should precede (3).

Next fixes in the definition (not applied, to keep the routed files consistent with `board.py`): copper + via keep-out over NT1
(Freerouting does not see net-tie polygons), the 6 pin-to-pin "violations" Freerouting reports on U1 (SOT-23-8 0.15 mm gaps + 25 um
DSN margin) are harmless.

## Tool defects found (tools/eda not edited; worked around in the board definition)
1. Plane fan-out places vias with the via net's class clearance, not the neighbour's → SAFETY_CHAIN DRC clearance lowered to 0.2 mm.
2. Plane fan-out ignores footprint-internal rule areas (Bosch LGA keep-out) and net-tie copper polygons → local IMU footprint without
   rule area + board via keep-out; NT1 moved and `zone_connect="none"`; Freerouting likewise routed a via through NT1's polygon.
3. A footprint-only rule area on B.Cu (no tracks/vias/copper ban) is exported to the DSN as a full B.Cu keep-out → removed once the
   placement was frozen.
4. KiCad's DRC report lists at most 499 unconnected items, so `summary.json` → `unconnected` saturates at 499 (true count 802 here,
   1720 ratsnest edges before routing); the summary should use the connectivity count.
5. Post stage crash in `_remove_unused_escapes` (above) and the host then running the finisher on an un-routed board.
6. Auto-arrange is weak on dense two-sided boards (69 passives > 20 mm from their anchor pin); with escape keep-aways it can fail
   ("no room") when passives are asked to stay on the IC's side.
