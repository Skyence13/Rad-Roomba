# RR-HEAD-ACQ rev A — circuit description

Reads with `circuit.py` (the complete net-level definition; `python3 circuit.py` prints the self-check) and
`electronics/analysis/head_afe_budget.md` (the numbers; "budget §n" below). Evidence labels as in the budget.
1085 part records, 1008 fitted purchased parts, 97 ICs; placed area ≈ 25 % of the board.

Reference scheme: channel c (0…7 = F0…F3, R0…R3) uses designators 100·(c+1)+k, lane n uses 1000+100·n+k, board blocks 2000+100·i+k.
The four lanes are generated by the same functions and the self-check proves they are identical after renaming.

## 2. Lane block (LANE; `lane()`, `lane_shield()`)

Trim buffers (TLV9062); latch clocks = comparator OR LATCH_TEST (74LVC32A + 74LVC1G32); five D latches with D = 1 (three 74LVC74A, one flip-flop spare and tied off):
BUSY = LLD latch (non-retriggerable until CLR), ULD, ALPHA for the floor chain, BUSY and ULD for the reference chain; one CLR line per chain with a 10 kΩ pull-down so that the latches are held clear in reset;
47 Ω source resistors and 100 kΩ pull-downs on the lines to the MCU. Lane monitor mux (TMUX1108): floor fast, reference fast, floor slow, reference preamp; second page floor preamp and baseline.
Auxiliary pigtail connector (JST GH-6): NTC at the SiPM carriers (10.0 kΩ 0.1 % to the 3.0 V reference), optional LED pulser, 3.3 V for an active sensor.
Shield: 12 clips and one formed can per lane (prototype housing is unshielded).

## 3. Bias (BIAS)

TPS61391 boost (from 3.3 V; integrated current mirror and 2 mA output limit) → RC → mirror → BSS123 follower in a 6 Hz precision loop (OPA2333, MMBTA42) → HV_BUS = 25 × HV_SET.
Reasons: the mandated boost → filter → linear regulator with limit and monitor, with a pass element that needs no high-voltage op-amp and fails low: with HV_SET = 0 the loop drives the bias to zero.
The boost output and the over-voltage comparator are strapped per SiPM family (budget §5.2) so that neither a shorted pass transistor nor a wrong DAC code can exceed the family limit.
Permit latch (74LVC74A) + NAND (74LVC00A): bias only after a rising edge of HV_EN with no inhibit (over-voltage, +5V_A low, lid open); bleeder and soft-start preset.
Second divider and buffer give an independent BIAS_VMON. The LT-series alternatives considered (LT8331, LT3042, LTC6102, ADA4895) were dropped because none of their data could be opened from this machine; every architecture-defining IC is a TI part whose datasheet was read.

## 4. DACs and baseline (DAC)

Four DAC60508Z exactly as the firmware map: U_DAC0 (U2301) floor thresholds lanes 0/1 + HV_SET; U_DAC1 (U2302) lanes 2/3 + PULSER_AMPL; U_DAC2 reference thresholds; U_DAC3 trims.
The two firmware "SPARE" outputs: U_DAC0 OUT7 = LED amplitude (harmless at zero), U_DAC1 OUT7 = test pad. Each DAC uses its internal 2.5 V reference (2 ppm/°C typ, 5 max — *datasheet*).
Baseline 0.600 V = buffered U_DAC1 reference × 3.16/13.16: no firmware action needed and it tracks a threshold reference. Only U_DAC0's SDO is wired to MISO until the SDO high-impedance behaviour is confirmed.

## 5. MCU and housekeeping (MCU)

STM32G474VET6, pin for pin from `firmware/head/pinmap.yaml` (checked by the self-check: 92 pins, no mismatch). 24 MHz TCXO into PF0 through 33 Ω. TC2030 pads, debug UART, two LEDs,
two TMP117, EUI EEPROM, optional TCA9534 expander. ADC reference: REF3030 (3.0 V) fed from 3.3 V so that it and the supervisors are valid before +5V_A.

Use of the six firmware spare pins (firmware may ignore all of them; each has a defined idle level):

| Pin | Board function | If firmware never drives it |
|---|---|---|
| PE15 (ADC4_IN2) | `IMON_CH`: per-channel anode current, selected by MON_MUX_A[2:0] | unused analogue input |
| PF2, PF9, PF10 | `PULSER_A0…A2`: channel that receives the test pulse | pulled low → channel F0 |
| PC12 | `LED_STROBE` | pulled low, LED pulser idle |
| PF1 | `SYNC_IN` from the DNP hardware-sync receiver | pulled low |

## 6. Communication (COMM_ISOLATED)

ISO1430B half-duplex isolated transceiver, bus side powered from BUS_5V (J2), no DC-DC in the head; SM712 line TVS, 120 Ω termination, no bias (chassis end has it).
DE and /RE tied together (pinmap). J2501 = JST GH-8, pin n = contract J2 pin n; the J2_PRES link is in the plug (0 Ω DNP on the board as an alternative). Sync receiver and isolator footprints DNP (contract).

## 7. Power (POWER_IN, POWER_REG)

J2001 = 8-way Micro-Fit, pin n = contract J1 pin n. 4.70 kΩ presence resistor and TVS on the cable side, common-mode choke, TPS26600 eFuse (reverse polarity, 8.0/8.8 V UVLO, 17.5 V OVP, 1.0 A limit, inrush control), π filter,
LMR51606 buck to 5.63 V at 1.1 MHz forced PWM (fixed frequency, no burst spectrum in the slow-shaper band), then LC + TPS7A2050 for +5V_A, LC + TLV76733 for +3V3_EVT, TLV76733 for +3V3.
One switching pre-regulator and three linear regulators cost about 0.4 W against two bucks, and that heat is welcome in this head; total 1.2 W typ / 2.1 W max (budget §8).
The interlock loop is a potential-free series chain J1.5 → three rail-switch connectors → 1.00 kΩ 0.1 % → J1.6; the zone read-back inputs (pinmap) use a separate auxiliary contact per zone.

## 8. Heater (HEATER)

P-channel high-side switch with Miller-capacitor slew limiting, 10 mΩ shunt + INA180A2, low-side bimetal cut-out on the heater assembly with a continuity/status circuit that works in both switch states, heater NTC input. The heater return never touches ground on this board (budget §9).

## 9. Monitor and selects (MONITOR)

74HC238 enables one lane mux from MON_MUX_A[1:0]; A2 selects floor/reference, A3 fast/alternate inside the lane mux; unity-gain OPA836 drives J2701 through 49.9 Ω and the ADC through 1 kΩ/100 pF.
74LVC138 selects the pulsed channel. A third TMUX1108 selects the per-channel current monitor.

## 11. Deviations and open points

See `CONTRACT_CHANGE_REQUESTS.md` and the review checklist. In short: bias full scale is 62.5 V with family straps (qualified range 20–60 V); zone read-back needs auxiliary contacts the baseline switches do not have;
J1 connector height; frame bond depends on whether the head frame touches the chassis; no humidity sensor.
