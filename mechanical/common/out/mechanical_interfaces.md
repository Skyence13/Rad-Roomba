# Mechanical interfaces between the head, prototype-body and production-body work packages

Generated by `mechanical/common/gen_interfaces.py` from `params.py` (contract 0.4.0-draft8). base_link mm unless stated. Design values (calculated / tool-checked by `layout.py`). **Import the named parameters; do not retype these numbers.** If an interface has to move, change `params.py` through the mechanical lead and rerun `build.sh`.

## 0. Who owns what

| Work package | Owns | Must not change |
|---|---|---|
| Head (both builds), `mechanical/head/` | everything inside the head envelope x 66.0..212.5, |y| <= 175, z 5.0..45.0, plus the two shoes, the latch keeper, the pigtails up to the J plugs | optical stack below z 28.5, board outline/holes, shoe section and datums, port positions |
| Prototype body (X1C), `mechanical/prototype_x1c/` | printed shells, chassis plate and front beam, receiver channels, latch, bridge, grip, battery bay and cassette, sensor and panel mounts | skin law (`P.BODY_ROWS`), running-gear datums, every position in sections 2-7 |
| Production body, `mechanical/production/` | the same parts as CNC aluminium + MJF PA12 | the same interfaces; a part may change material and wall, not its interface |

Both body builds must accept both head builds and both must pass `layout.py` with their detailed parts substituted for the envelopes.

## 8. Shared rules

- Gaskets: closed-cell silicone rated -40 C on every joint named in `mech_calcs.md` section 7; membrane vents and drains as listed there.
- Fasteners into printed parts: heat-set inserts, M3 minimum for structure; no thread forming in printed polymer for anything that is serviced.
- Anything wider than the tyres (295 mm) on the body, or outside the head envelope on the head, needs the lead's agreement (swept volume and wall strips).
- A prototype part may be thicker than its production twin inward only: outer skin and every interface in this file stay identical.
- Open items the downstream engineers must not design around silently: wheel/hub selection, caster sealing, receptacle and switch dimensions marked UNVERIFIED in `parts.yaml`, LiDAR scan-plane height.
