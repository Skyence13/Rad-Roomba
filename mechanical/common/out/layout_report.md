## 3. Rev D containment (calculated) -- why a body change is proposed

- Rev D inner cavity 2605 cm3; hand well 704 cm3 and wheel wells 65 cm3 leave about **1836 cm3**.
- Hardware envelopes (no structure, harness or clearance): **2144 cm3** -> battery cassette 9 Ah 1172, RR-CHS-MAIN with height zones 362, CM5 + carrier 114, 2 gearmotors (swept box) 259, battery coupler 39, E-stop, selector, 3 buttons (rear bodies) 177, J1, J2, inlet rear bodies 21.
- Rev D inner width 213.8 mm (191.4 mm between wheel wells), inner height 76 mm.
- Rev D motor centreline z = 52 puts a 37 mm can from z 33.5 to 70.5: the 160 x 100 board can only lie above z 75, where the Rev D cavity is <= 2 x 73 mm wide at the board corners and the LiDAR/E-stop stand.
- The 151 x 99 x 65 mm pack needs a 157 x 107 x 70 mm bay behind the axle; that plan area is the hand well (floor z 42.7).
- Free cavity volume is smaller than the sum of the hardware envelopes before any structure, harness or clearances.

## 9. Carry-grip load path

Hand -> transverse grip bar (25 x 22 mm, 168 mm span, above the calculated centre of gravity) -> two cheeks (14 x 25 mm) -> cheek legs outside the main-board edges -> chassis plate between the motor mounts -> motor mounts/wheels and battery bay. The printed shells carry no lifting load. Bar and cheek stresses are in out/mech_calcs.md.

## 10. Alternative purchased parts (calculated from the verified envelopes)

- 12 Ah pack PSL-SC-12120 (151 x 99 x 101 mm, 1.65 kg): same length and width footprint class as the 9 Ah pack lying down (151 x 99), but 34 mm taller in either orientation. Cassette top would be z = 144 against the tunnel roof at z = 113, the lowest inner skin over the bay at z = 112 and the LiDAR base at z = 136: **does not fit the proposed body** (short by 35 mm of height at the tail). Fitting it means raising the tail deck and LiDAR by that amount (or a height scale of about 1.55 instead of 1.30). Decision needed with the power budget (it would also supply most of the tail ballast).
- SICK picoScan150 (60 x 60 x 82 mm, 0.22 kg): same footprint as the LakiBeam 1L pad, 2 mm taller -> top at z = 218; fits without layout change (system-plug exit to be checked).
- 5 mm rail insert: head envelope z min becomes 5.0 (= contract minimum 5.0); run `python -m mechanical.common.layout --rail 5` to regenerate the model with it.
