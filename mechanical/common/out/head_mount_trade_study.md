# Head mount trade study (DEC-041)

Generated by `python -m mechanical.common.calc.run_all` (module `calc/mount_trade.py`, contract 0.4.0-draft9). Every number is **calculated** with the same models as `out/mech_calcs.md` (mount = four bearing lands with one assumed contact stiffness per land, 1500 N/mm printed / 6000 N/mm aluminium, plus beam theory for arms and wings; three-point gap kinematics; rigid-body balance with the 12 Ah baseline pack). Nothing is measured; the ranking is robust to the assumed contact stiffness because it scales every option equally.

Fixed in every option: the head interior (four 80 x 100 windows, 87.5 pitch, film z = 10, stack-up and RR-HEAD-ACQ as in `board_envelopes.yaml`), head top z = 45, body Rev E, track 270, caster at -204.

## Options

- **A** Rev D single central keyed neck: steel tongue in a socket collar on the head centre line, two bearing lands at y = +-31, captive cross pin; row at x = 204.
- **B** Wide twin-arm receiver: two arms on the head at the lane seams (y = +-87.5) slide into two channels under a chassis front beam, one captive draw latch on the centre line; row stays at x = 204, so the arms bridge 73 mm of free span.
- **C** As B with the overhang removed: the head moves rearward until its wing returns are 17 mm ahead of the tyres; row at x = 131.2, the arms shrink to shoes that are fully inside the channels. The nose loses its lower 'chin' (x > 62, z < 50) and overhangs the head by 36 mm.
- **D** Floor-following head: head carried by its own rollers/skids, joined to the chassis by a pivoting link; evaluated with rollers on the row centre line at both ends of the head (the arrangement that best decouples the gap).

## Evaluation of D

A floor-following head removes the cantilever from the balance (tip margin +72 mm) and makes the row-centre gap independent of chassis pitch. It does not remove gap error: debris or a seam under a roller moves that end 1:1 (rigid option C: 1.61 mm per mm of single-wheel drop, and only when a wheel meets the feature), the window edges still follow the pivot (0.12 mm per mm), and the head needs a travel limit and a lift for reversing and for the 3/5 mm rail trials. Its decisive drawback for this project is radiological: the rollers run on floor before it is counted (in the adjacent pass or in reverse), can pick up and redeposit loose uranium-series dust, and add the least cleanable hardware next to the windows. It also needs a new calibration concept and widens the head or shadows the outer lanes. **Not recommended now.** Keep it as the fallback if the floor-rig trials (`out/gap_measurement_method.md`) show that a rigid mount cannot hold the calibrated gap interval on real trailer floors.

## Recommendation: option C (implemented in `params.py`, layout and all calculations)

- **Balance**: forward tip margin +19.2 mm (A) -> **+22.7 mm (C)** with the 12 Ah pack and the heaviest head, static caster load 12.2 -> 13.6 N; with the 9 Ah pack +9.8 -> +13.5 mm. No ballast in any configuration.
- **Stiffness**: mount pitch 864 -> 4374 N m/rad and roll 5766 -> 45938 N m/rad (printed); 20 N at the front outer corner 0.74 -> **0.19 mm** printed, 0.14 -> 0.04 mm aluminium; first mode 45 -> 84 Hz printed.
- **Gap**: pitch sensitivity at the row centre 1.00 -> **0.64 mm per mm** at the caster; worst rail corner per mm of single-wheel drop 1.79 -> 1.61; the 3 mm rail touches at 3.9 -> 4.3 mm features, the 5 mm rail at 2.8 -> 3.1 mm.
- **Envelope**: overall length 537 -> 464 mm; outer head corner radius 318 -> 260 mm (the tail, 278 mm, now sets the swept circle); corner speed at the yaw limit 0.095 -> 0.078 m/s.
- **Build**: no sculpted neck; each printed half-head has its own bearing shoe on a seam rib; one visible glove-operable latch; receiver channels bolt to the same front beam as the motor mounts (short load path head -> wheels).
- **B** has the same receiver but keeps the 204 mm overhang: it gains stiffness in roll and loses some in sag through the arms, and leaves balance and gap sensitivity as in A. It is only preferable if the nose may not be changed.
