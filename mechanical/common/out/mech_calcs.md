Worst operating case: production, 9 Ah PSL-SC-1290 (1.00 kg), heaviest / dummy head (production + 15 %) -> margin **-6.9 mm**, static caster load 0.5 N (0.6 % of weight). The Rev D body with the same hardware would be nose-heavy: the cantilevered head (1.78 kg at x = 199 mm) is why the Rev E body grows rearward only and carries the pack, compute, LiDAR and controls behind the axle.
To keep >= 8 % of the weight on the caster under braking on the 5 deg down-slope in every operating configuration, add **0.85 kg tail ballast** at x = -250 mm (inside the tail-cap hatch) -> worst caster fraction 8.4 %. The 12 Ah pack provides most of this by itself.
Main assumptions: masses marked estimate/UNVERIFIED above (+-15 % on structure moves x_CG by about +-4 mm); rigid tyres; weigh all three contacts on the first build (VT-032).

**Reading.** Positive values are downward at the named point. The wings themselves are stiff (short, deep); the receiver joint and, if the cover is not a shear panel, wing twist dominate the 20 N corner case. Design rules for the head engineers: (1) screw the cover to the frame at <= 40 mm pitch so the spreader-cover cell is closed; (2) the spreader must be one piece across y = 0 and bolted to both frame halves; (3) receiver stiffness >= 1500 N m/rad in pitch is the number to prove by test (20 N at the front corner then moves it < 1 mm).

Vehicle heave on the tyres: with an assumed 40 N/mm per tyre and 9 kg, f = 15 Hz (assumption; solid-tyre stiffness must be measured). All head modes are well above it and above the seam-crossing excitation.

**Gap band (calculated).** For a floor whose single features stay within 2 mm (seam step, debris under one wheel, cross-member dishing over the wheelbase) the film gap at the outer corners stays within **5.0 .. 15.0 mm** including the static worst case (6.5 .. 13.5 mm without it). The most sensitive situation is one drive wheel dropping while the same-side front corner leads (1.79 mm per mm). The 3 mm rail (7 mm clearance) touches at a 3.9 mm single feature (3.1 mm with the static band used up); the 5 mm rail (5 mm clearance) at 2.8 mm (1.9 mm). A rail touch opens HEAD_LOOP, so these are nuisance-stop thresholds as well as protection thresholds: on a 3 mm seam the 3 mm rail keeps about 3 mm of clearance, while the 5 mm rail keeps about 1 mm nominally and touches once the static band or an oblique crossing (one wheel first) is included. Floor crown is negligible (5 mm sag over a 2.4 m wide floor changes the corner gap by < 0.1 mm). Dynamic terms (braking pitch on 40 N/mm tyres < 0.05 mm, receiver bounce at 0.5 g < 0.1 mm) are small against the kinematic terms; they must be confirmed on the rig.

The measurement method that replaces these assumptions with data is in `out/gap_measurement_method.md`.

### Condensation management provisions (layout decisions)

- **Gasket lines**: one perimeter seal at the shell split (z = 63.7), O-ring/flat gaskets at every panel device, the saddle service cover, the tail-cap hatch and the head cover; closed-cell silicone foam rated -40 C.
- **Breather**: one ePTFE membrane vent (M12 class) in the lower hull at the rear right quarter and one in the head rear wall beside the J2 port, so pressure equalises on the warm-room return without pumping moist air past the seals.
- **Desiccant**: 10 g replaceable pack with humidity indicator inside the saddle cover (visible when the cover is lifted) and a 5 g pack in the head chimney; change on indicator.
- **Drain paths**: the bay floor is the chassis plate; two 3 mm drain holes with duck-bill valves at the lowest points beside the caster bracket; the battery cassette sits on 2 mm ribs so melt water runs under it; the LiDAR pad and grip saddle slope outward; no pocket faces upward without a drain.
- **Head**: the window films are the cold face; the heater on the spreader keeps the optical stack above the film temperature so condensation forms outside, not between film and screen; the chimney is the low point and gets the membrane vent.
- **Warm-room return**: bag or cover the robot before entering the warm room, keep it closed until the bay NTC is within 5 K of the room (about 2 time constants = 181 min for the body, 94 min for the head), keep the heaters off and the electronics on during the soak; charge only above +5 C pack temperature (contract).
- **Connectors**: J1/J2 and the charge inlet face upward-forward / outward and are capped when unmated; receptacles are above the split line so pooled water cannot reach them.
