|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Gap band (calculated).** For a floor whose single features stay within 2 mm (seam step, debris under one wheel, cross-member dishing over the wheelbase) the film gap at the outer corners stays within **5.3 .. 14.7 mm** including the static worst case (6.8 .. 13.2 mm without it). The most sensitive situation is one drive wheel dropping while the same-side front corner leads (1.61 mm per mm). The 3 mm rail (7 mm clearance) touches at a 4.3 mm single feature (3.4 mm with the static band used up); the 5 mm rail (5 mm clearance) at 3.1 mm (2.1 mm). A rail touch opens HEAD_LOOP, so these are nuisance-stop thresholds as well as protection thresholds: on a 3 mm seam the 3 mm rail keeps about 3 mm of clearance, while the 5 mm rail keeps about 1 mm nominally and touches once the static band or an oblique crossing (one wheel first) is included. Floor crown is negligible (5 mm sag over a 2.4 m wide floor changes the corner gap by < 0.1 mm). Dynamic terms (braking pitch on 40 N/mm tyres < 0.05 mm, receiver bounce at 0.5 g < 0.1 mm) are small against the kinematic terms; they must be confirmed on the rig.

The measurement method that replaces these assumptions with data is in `out/gap_measurement_method.md`.

### Condensation management provisions (layout decisions)

- **Gasket lines**: one perimeter seal at the shell split (z = 63.7), O-ring/flat gaskets at every panel device, the saddle service cover, the tail-cap hatch and the head cover; closed-cell silicone foam rated -40 C.
- **Breather**: one ePTFE membrane vent (M12 class) in the lower hull at the rear right quarter and one in the head rear wall beside the J2 port, so pressure equalises on the warm-room return without pumping moist air past the seals.
- **Desiccant**: 10 g replaceable pack with humidity indicator inside the saddle cover (visible when the cover is lifted) and a 5 g pack in the head chimney; change on indicator.
- **Drain paths**: the bay floor is the chassis plate; two 3 mm drain holes with duck-bill valves at the lowest points beside the caster bracket; the battery cassette sits on 2 mm ribs so melt water runs under it; the LiDAR pad and grip saddle slope outward; no pocket faces upward without a drain.
- **Head**: the window films are the cold face; the heater on the spreader keeps the optical stack above the film temperature so condensation forms outside, not between film and screen; the chimney is the low point and gets the membrane vent.
- **Warm-room return**: bag or cover the robot before entering the warm room, keep it closed until the bay NTC is within 5 K of the room (about 2 time constants = 181 min for the body; head: see the head report), keep the heaters off and the electronics on during the soak; charge only above +5 C pack temperature (contract).
- **Connectors**: J1/J2 and the charge inlet face upward-forward / outward and are capped when unmated; receptacles are above the split line so pooled water cannot reach them.
