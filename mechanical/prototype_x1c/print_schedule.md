# RR-X print schedule (Bambu Lab X1 Carbon, 0.4 mm hardened nozzle)

Generated by `rrx/docs.py` from the model. **Nothing in this file was sliced**: per the owner's instruction slicing is done by the owner in Bambu Studio from the exports. Every printed part is exported **in its print orientation, +Z up, lowest face on z = 0, bounding-box corner at the origin** as `out/step/<part>.step`, `out/stl/<part>.stl` and `out/3mf/<part>.3mf`. Bed-fit is a bounding-box check (tool-checked) against 250 x 250 mm (3 mm edge margin on the 256 mm plate) and 253 mm height; the stock 0.4 mm preset prints 250 mm high. Mass is CAD volume x density x effective fill (calculated estimate); time is a rule-of-thumb from the mass, not a slicer figure.

| ASA | Bambu ASA @BBL X1C 0.4 nozzle | 0.2 mm / 4 / 20 % gyroid | Textured PEI Plate | Skins, covers, cassette, trays. UV-stable (outdoor grade), tolerates the wipe-down agents expected (water/detergent, 70 % IPA short contact, dilute hypochlorite; NOT ketones/esters), glass transition ~100 C so heat-set inserts and a sun-heated trailer are safe, lower density than PETG-CF. Unfilled, so it keeps some ductility at -25 C (notched impact falls to roughly half of the +23 C value: handbook trend, UNVERIFIED for the spool -> drop/impact coupons at -25 C are a first-article test). Needs the closed chamber and a brim on the tall pieces (warp). |
| PAHT-CF | Bambu PAHT-CF @BBL X1C | 0.2 mm / 5 / 35 % gyroid | Textured PEI Plate | Load-bearing printed parts (motor pods, wheel rims, bulkhead, hatch frame, cassette handle, cliff pods, service cover). Highest stiffness and creep resistance of the stock X1C materials, low moisture uptake for a polyamide, keeps strength at -25 C. Carbon-filled: electrically conductive surface -> not used for the cassette or anything touching terminals. Print dry (80 C / 8 h), layer adhesion in Z is about half of XY -> every part is oriented so the service load is in-plane. |
| TPU95A | Bambu TPU 95A HF @BBL X1C | 0.2 mm / 4 / 20 % gyroid | Textured PEI Plate | Tyres only. 95A grips painted steel/plywood when warm; it stiffens markedly below about -10 C and approaches its glass transition near -30 C (polyester grades earlier) -> traction and compliance at -20/-25 C are NOT assumed, see the wheel caveats in the report. |

General: closed chamber for ASA and PAHT-CF; glue stick on the textured PEI for PAHT-CF; brim 5 mm on every part taller than 80 mm or with a contact patch under 25 cm2; seam at the rear/inside; heat-set inserts after printing (see `assembly.md`). Supports = tree (auto) only where the table says so.
