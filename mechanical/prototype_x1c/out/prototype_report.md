**Printed with metal lands or fabricated aluminium?** Decision: the chassis plate (4 mm 6061, laser cut) with the front beam in one piece, and two **machined 6061 channels** with hardened 4 mm dowel pins as the replaceable z lands. Reasons (calculated/handbook): (1) stiffness: the common model assumes 6000 N/mm per aluminium land against 1500 N/mm printed, i.e. four times the pitch stiffness and half the rattle-mode amplitude at the row; a printed PAHT-CF channel also halves its modulus when moist. (2) wear: aluminium shoes sliding on a printed slot with grit abrade the datum; a steel dowel is a line contact that does not pick up grit and is replaced by pressing it out. (3) creep: the latch preload sits on the x-stop pads permanently. (4) the part is a prismatic block: one slot milled from below, four reamed cross holes, five tapped/doweled holes. A printed copy of the same geometry (`out/step/receiver_channel_fitcheck_*.step`) is exported for a fit check before metal is cut; it is not the datum build.

**Thermal path to the head (calculated estimate, IB-39 follow-up).** As designed, each receiver channel (RRX-F02L/R, machined 6061-T6) is bolted metal-to-metal under the 4 mm 6061 chassis plate (3 x M4 + 2 x 4 mm steel dowels, ear contact about 640 mm2 per channel): its conductance to the plate is of the order of several W/K, so the channel runs at plate temperature and the head's 'receiver metal at ambient' bound (heater about 43-66 W, `mechanical/head/out/thermal_summary.json`) applies rather than its 0.07 W/K-per-shoe assumption. Proposal, not built: a G10 shim under each channel's ears with the channel made shorter by the shim thickness (z datum C unchanged: the land pins stay at z 14 in the channel), G10 shoulder bushings on the M4 screws and the locating dowels in G10 sleeves. Conductance estimate: 1.5 mm shim 0.3 W/m K x 640 mm2 / 1.5 mm = 0.13 W/K plus about 0.04 W/K through three A2 screws, about 0.17 W/K per channel (0.11 W/K in series with the shoe's 0.3 W/K isolator); a 3 mm shim gives about 0.10 W/K per channel (0.07 W/K in series), matching the head assumption. It changes the head datum stack by a compliant layer: re-check the land-height tolerance (+0.04/-0.02 mm) and the latch preload creep before adopting it.

## 9. Wheel (design item: no 104 x 25 catalogue wheel)

Printed PAHT-CF rim (86 mm, 8 drive keys) bolted from outboard to a purchased 6 mm set-screw hub (Pololu #1999 class, UNVERIFIED dimensions) that is fixed on the D shaft first; printed TPU 95A tyre 9 mm thick, keyed and bonded, rolling diameter 104.0 mm unloaded (contract). Caveats: (1) TPU 95A stiffens strongly below about -10 C and loses grip on frost; the contract friction values are not demonstrated cold.
