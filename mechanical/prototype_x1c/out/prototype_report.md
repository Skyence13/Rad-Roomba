**Printed with metal lands or fabricated aluminium?** Decision: the chassis plate (4 mm 6061, laser cut, two 1.5 mm pockets milled from below per channel) with the front beam in one piece, and two **machined 6061 channels** with hardened 4 mm dowel pins as the replaceable z lands. Reasons (calculated/handbook): (1) stiffness: the common model assumes 6000 N/mm per aluminium land against 1500 N/mm printed, i.e. four times the pitch stiffness and half the rattle-mode amplitude at the row; a printed PAHT-CF channel also halves its modulus when moist. (2) wear: aluminium shoes sliding on a printed slot with grit abrade the datum; a steel dowel is a line contact that does not pick up grit and is replaced by pressing it out. (3) creep: the latch preload sits on the x-stop pads permanently. (4) the part is a prismatic block: one slot milled from below, four reamed cross holes, tapped and pinned ears. A printed copy of the same geometry (`out/step/receiver_channel_fitcheck_*.step`) is exported for a fit check before metal is cut; it is not the datum build.

**Receiver thermal break (DEC-045 / IB-64, built).** Each channel (RRX-F02L/R) hangs under the plate on a ground 4.0 mm G-10 shim (RRX-F12L/R) under its ears; no metal part crosses the shim: the three M4 x 18 screws sit in G-10 top-hat bushings in 6.0 mm plate holes (DIN 6796 spring washer + ISO 7089 washer under the head, 1500 N, 1.2 N m); two lower 4 m6 x 8 pins pressed into the ear engage the shim only (top 1.5 mm below the plate); two upper pins pressed flush into the plate pass through the shim and end in a 6.5 x 1.5 relief in the ear. The channel ears and walls stop at z 29.0 (plate - shim); the roof exists only as two bridges over the land stations, which stand up into 1.5 mm pockets in the plate underside with a 1.5 mm air gap. Datum C (z 16.0) and the land pins are unchanged (tool-checked, section 5 rows). Conductance channel -> plate (calculated, G-10 0.30 W/m K handbook class, UNVERIFIED): **0.081 W/K per channel** against the 0.10 W/K target; the joint it replaces was about 6.5 W/K. ## 9. Wheel (design item: no 104 x 25 catalogue wheel)

Printed PAHT-CF rim (86 mm, 8 drive keys) bolted from outboard to a purchased 6 mm set-screw hub (Pololu #1999 class, UNVERIFIED dimensions) that is fixed on the D shaft first; printed TPU 95A tyre 9 mm thick, keyed and bonded, rolling diameter 104.0 mm unloaded (contract). Caveats: (1) TPU 95A stiffens strongly below about -10 C and loses grip on frost; the contract friction values are not demonstrated cold. (2) A 9 mm solid tyre under 33..36 N per wheel compresses by a few tenths of a millimetre and creeps when parked cold -> the film gap calibration must be done loaded and after a cold soak. (3) Printed TPU has layer lines across the tread: tread wear and diameter must be re-measured after the first hours. (4) Alternative kept open: the same rim turned down to carry a purchased 86 x 9 polyurethane tyre/O-ring band (60..70A cold-grade) if the cold traction test fails. The shaft eccentricity (shaft 7 mm below the can axis, UNVERIFIED) is handled in the pod: can bore on z = 59, shaft boss hole on z = 52, and twelve M3 holes so the UNVERIFIED six-hole pattern fits in either clocking.
