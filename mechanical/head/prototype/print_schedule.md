# Print schedule -- prototype head (Bambu Lab X1 Carbon, stock 256 x 256 x 256 mm)

Generated by `mechanical/head/docs.py` from `plates.yaml` and `out/checks.json`. **Not sliced**: the owner imports the print-orientation STEP files (`prototype/print/*.step`, Z up, part already on z = 0) into Bambu Studio and slices there. Fit and overhangs are tool-checked on the oriented solids (checks.py); slicer behaviour is not.

## Materials and cold-impact reasoning

- **PA-CF** -- PAHT-CF (PA12 base): polyamide 12 is the usual choice for cold-impact parts and keeps a useful notched impact strength well below -20 C (handbook behaviour of PA12, not tested here; the filament maker's cold data is not verified); carbon fibre lowers elongation, so no snap fits are made from it. Used for every part that carries a rail, latch, screw or insert load.
- **PETG-CF** -- PETG-CF: glass transition about 80 C, but noticeably more notch-sensitive in the cold than PA-CF; only for flat, lightly loaded parts that are clamped or bonded (cassettes, lane rings, barrier) and for the transport covers, which flex once per use at room temperature.
- **TPU** -- TPU 95A: stays elastic down to about -40 C (its Tg is far below zero), which is what a cable grommet needs in the cold.

All PA-CF filament is dried (80 C, 8 h) before printing and the parts are stored sealed; the structure calculation uses both the dry and the conditioned modulus.

## Critical surfaces (no support marks, no seam)

- Frame halves: the underside (bed face) is the cassette seat and film-plane datum -> first-layer flow / elephant-foot compensation on, no brim on the cassette lands (5 mm outer brim only); module seat shoulders (z 11.0), rib tops (z 29.4 / 30.0), spreader seat (z 30.0), joint faces (y = 0), lid boss tops (z 43.0). None of them faces down in print orientation (tool-checked list above), so the slicer never puts support on them. Seam position: rear wall outside.
- Guide-rod bores print as 4.3 mm horizontal holes and are reamed 4.3 H9 after printing; dowel holes reamed 3.0 H8; heat-set inserts after reaming.
- Rail inserts lie on their contact face (smooth bed side outward). Cassettes print flat: the film land is the top surface -> ironing on.

## After printing

1. Heat-set inserts (M2.5: 38, M3: 18) at 230-250 C with a flat tip, flush to 0.1 below the face; check perpendicularity with a screw.
2. Light test before assembly: each frame half over a 1000 lm lamp in a dark room, look for pinholes in the 1.5 mm outer wall and the 3.25 mm half rib (contingency: black foil tape inside; L20 in head_report.md).
3. Dry-fit the halves on the dowels: joint gap <= 0.1 mm along the full length.
