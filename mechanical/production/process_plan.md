## Sealing approach

- Lid to lower hull: one closed-cell silicone perimeter gasket on the hull tongue land (the only skin joint left), compressed by the M3 perimeter screws with EPDM bonded washers. The x 47, x -38, x -71 and y 0 joints of the X1C build no longer exist, so their gaskets, RTV beads and dowels are gone.
- Saddle cover, hatch door, charge inlet: unchanged gasket lands (cover gasket, hatch sponge strip, inlet cover).
- Devices in the skin (ToF windows, controls, receptacles, LiDAR): the prototype seats unchanged (flat gaskets / O-rings / bonded windows).
- Plate to hull: RTV bead at the sole ring as prototype; pods pass the skin through the sealed notches (O-ring cord).
- SLS PA11 is porous at the surface: dye + 2K PU topcoat seals it; gasket lands are left uncoated but bead-blasted, flat to 0.3 over 100 mm (drawing).

## Cold-weather notes (-25 C objective)

- **Target: -25 C objective (RQ-029), -20 C operating.** Every value below is a typical data-sheet class value, UNVERIFIED for the purchased grade.
- **Skins and small parts, SLS PA11:** keeps notched impact and elongation far better than PA12 or ASA at -25 C (PA11 glass transition about 40-45 C, but the amorphous phase stays ductile well below -30 C in the usual data); avoid sharp internal corners at screw bosses (R >= 1 mm), no thin snap fits below 1.5 mm. Topcoat: 2K PU (flexible grade) for UV; no brittle lacquer.
- **Tyres, cast PU:** polyether-based PU (not polyester: hydrolysis, and polyester grades stiffen more in the cold); 85 Shore A at +23 C rises by roughly 5-10 Shore A at -25 C (UNVERIFIED), so the gap budget's tyre term (+-0.5 mm) is re-measured cold; glass transition must be below -40 C (supplier datum to obtain).
- **Seals:** closed-cell silicone (perimeter, cover, hatch) and silicone O-ring cord, -55 C rated; EPDM bonded washers on the perimeter screws (EPDM -40 C). No NBR/neoprene (stiffens and loses set below -20 C). RTV: neutral-cure silicone, -50 C.
- **Caster:** TENTE wheel as in the prototype: -20 C limit with a plain bearing (VERIFIED data-sheet limit, parts.yaml) -- below the -25 C objective. Production action: low-temperature grease and a ball-bearing variant, or a replacement caster rated -30 C (to be selected; UNVERIFIED).
- **Gearmotors and hubs:** gearbox grease viscosity rises in the cold (drive_cold budget in the power model); the M3 hub screws keep medium thread-lock (works to -55 C).
- **G-10 shim and bushings:** NEMA G-10 is rated to cryogenic use; the through-thickness CTE mismatch to aluminium is in the datum-C stack (mech_calcs.md section 9).
- **Windows:** PMMA IR-pass windows stay as in the prototype; PMMA is notch-sensitive in the cold, so the production bezel keeps the window recessed 1.1 mm and bonded with flexible silicone (no hard clamp).
- **Battery:** pack discharge floor -10 C at the skin, pre-heat to +5 C before charge (contract thermal policy; mech_calcs.md section 7).
